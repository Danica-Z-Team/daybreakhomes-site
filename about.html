Our Story

We started Daybreak Homes because Daybreak deserves real estate guidance that actually knows the neighborhood: which villages back up to the lake, where the walk to SoDa Row is shortest, and what a home is really worth once you factor in the lifestyle around it.

We're not a national brand parachuting in. We're the Zander Real Estate Team, and Daybreak, South Jordan is our home base. From there, we serve buyers and sellers across Salt Lake County and Utah County who want that same kind of local, connected guidance.

Real estate, done well, is about people first, homes second. Building people, one home at a time isn't a tagline we picked for the website. It's how we work.
