Life in Daybreak

A lot of people talk about missing the feeling of community: knowing their neighbors, leaning on each other, having a "village" instead of only connecting through devices.

Every detail of Daybreak was masterplanned with that in mind. Front porches sit close enough to the sidewalk to invite a conversation, not just a wave, turning a walk to the mailbox into an actual exchange. Yards are smaller by design, because the parks, trails, and shared green space around them are bigger.

Designed to Get You Outside

Oquirrh Lake anchors the original Lower Villages, and the Watercourse does the same job for the Upper Villages. More than fifty miles of trail stitch it all together, alongside ongoing upgrades that keep those shared spaces worth walking through.

The Five-Minute Walk

If you've already looked into Daybreak, you may have come across the five-minute rule. It's simple: research shows most people will leave the car in the garage if where they're headed is within a five-minute walk. Daybreak's layout was planned around exactly that, with parks, restaurants, shops, schools, offices, and light rail placed within an easy walk or bike ride of home, connected by sidewalks, trails, and dedicated bike routes rather than just roads.

Schools Within Walking Distance

Many families end up driving further than they'd like, simply because of where a school happens to sit. In Daybreak, that trade-off mostly disappears. Several schools sit within walking distance of each neighborhood, connected by off-street trails and sidewalks, so a lot of kids walk instead of riding a bus. If you have kids, you know this matters! Public, private, and charter options have all opened here, and more are to come!

Downtown Daybreak & SoDa Row

Coffee shops, restaurants, and community events at SoDa Row give Daybreak a gathering place that feels like the center of the neighborhood, and it's only getting bigger as Downtown Daybreak and Center Field Row take shape nearby.

Parks and a Reason to Use Them

Daybreak was planned so there's at least one park within easy walking distance of every home, and the LiveDAYBREAK team keeps them busy with concerts, farmers markets, and community events throughout the year.
