Life in Daybreak

Daybreak is built around Oquirrh Lake, a network of walking and biking trails, and a collection of connected villages, Garden Park, SoDa Row, Highland Park, Eastlake, Creekside, and Founders Park among them. It's the kind of place that's designed for slowing down, even on a Tuesday.

Oquirrh Lake & Trails

Miles of paths wrap the lake and connect village to village, made for evening walks, weekend rides, and getting to know your neighbors along the way.

Walkable Villages

Garden Park, SoDa Row, Highland Park, Eastlake, and Creekside each have their own feel, from front-porch bungalows to lakeside townhomes.

SoDa Row & Community Life

Coffee shops, restaurants, and community events at SoDa Row give Daybreak a gathering place that feels like the center of the neighborhood.
