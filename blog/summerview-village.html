Housing and Daily Life in Summerview Village, Daybreak, UT for 2026

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

Summerview Village is one of the distinct residential sections inside the master-planned community of Daybreak in South Jordan, UT. It's not a standalone neighborhood with its own commercial strip - it's a carefully laid-out piece of a much larger puzzle, connected to the rest of the best neighborhoods in Daybreak through trails and green spaces rather than through strip malls and parking lots.

The median home price across the South Jordan market sits at around $630,000 right now. Summerview Village listings generally land in that same territory, with a mix of detached houses and attached townhomes available to buyers at different price points.

An Overview of Summerview Village

Daybreak organizes itself into distinct villages, and Summerview Village is one of them. The entire focus here is residential - don't come looking for a coffee shop on the corner, because there isn't one. What you'll find instead is a layout that rewards walking and biking, with sidewalks and paved trails running between homes and the shared green spaces that Daybreak is known for.

Buying here means tapping into the broader Daybreak amenity network. The trade-off is that you're also joining a community association - something buyers should understand clearly before they fall in love with a listing.

How the Neighborhood Connects to Daybreak

Membership in the Daybreak community association comes with the purchase. The HOA handles landscaping, snow removal on shared paths, and upkeep of the larger community structures - so you're not managing all of that yourself, but you are paying for it monthly.

Getting around South Jordan from here is straightforward. The local road network connects the village to the rest of the city, and the design intentionally pushes residents toward walking or biking to nearby community hubs rather than driving every single errand.

Real Estate Options and Home Prices

The South Jordan market median sits at roughly $630,000, with homes averaging about 50 days on market before closing. Those are the broad strokes. What you'll pay in Summerview Village depends on the specific property - its size, its construction year, and whether it's a detached house or an attached townhome.

One number buyers sometimes overlook until it's too late: the HOA fees. Whatever those dues are on a given property, your lender will fold them into your debt-to-income ratio. That affects what you qualify for, so factor them in early.

Home Types and Construction Styles

The architecture follows the Daybreak playbook - front porches, rear-entry garages. That setup keeps street fronts pedestrian-friendly rather than driveway-heavy, which is a real difference you notice once you're living there.

Inventory across South Jordan runs around 460 available homes, so buyers have room to look at multiple options before deciding. That said, recent sales data shows homes closing at nearly 99% of list price. There's space to shop, but not much space to lowball.

Dining and Daily Commutes

Summerview Village is residential through and through, which means you're driving or biking out of the neighborhood for groceries, dinner, and everything else. The road layout connects directly to South Jordan's major thoroughfares, and from there you can reach highways into downtown Salt Lake City or elsewhere in the valley without much trouble.

It's a reasonable setup if you know what you're signing up for. If you're expecting walkable retail at your doorstep, this isn't that.

Local Restaurants and Everyday Shopping

Daybreak's commercial centers are a short drive or bike ride from the village - casual dining, coffee shops, everyday retail. Nothing is inside the residential streets themselves.

One thing worth doing before you make an offer: map the daily commute from the specific listing, not just from the neighborhood in general. How deep a home sits inside the village affects how long it takes to get out to the main access roads, and that adds up over time.

Schools, Healthcare, and Local Weather

The Jordan School District serves all of Daybreak, including Summerview Village. Depending on where exactly a home sits within the community, students typically attend Daybreak Elementary - though boundary lines shift, and you should verify the current enrollment zones directly with the district before you buy. Early Light Academy, a public charter school, also serves local residents and is worth knowing about as you think through your options.

Medical Facilities and Hospitals

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center at 5126 W Daybreak Parkway covers everyday clinical needs and runs a 24-hour emergency room. For major trauma cases, the University of Utah Hospital - a Level I Trauma Center - and Intermountain Medical Center are the facilities residents rely on, though both require traveling outside the immediate area.

Climate and Seasonal Weather

Four seasons, and you'll feel all of them. Summers are hot and dry; winters bring real snow. The HOA handles snow removal on the primary community trails, but your driveway and immediate walkways are your responsibility.

Central air conditioning comes standard in essentially every home built in this community. During your inspection period, pay attention to the age and condition of the HVAC system - in this climate, that equipment works hard.

Finding a Home in the Village

Around 400 homes have sold recently across the South Jordan market. About 14% of those sales closed above asking price, which tells you that well-priced homes still draw competitive offers even with decent inventory. There's no guarantee of getting a deal just because there are options to look at.

An agent who knows the Daybreak master plan can help you track new inventory before it sits around long enough to get stale. They can also walk you through the actual differences in HOA coverage between a townhome and a detached house - and those differences matter more than most buyers expect.

Renting Versus Buying

Renting a townhome or house in Daybreak before buying is a legitimate strategy. It lets you road-test the commute and get a feel for the daily rhythms of the community before you're committed to a mortgage. Rental inventory fluctuates, but it's worth checking if you're on the fence.

When you're ready to buy, read the community's master deed and association rules before you close - not after. Those documents spell out homeowner responsibilities and building restrictions in detail, and there are no surprises in them if you've read them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Summerview Village in Daybreak known for?

Summerview Village is a residential section within the master-planned Daybreak community in South Jordan, UT. It features a mix of single-family homes and townhomes connected by paved pedestrian trails. The area is entirely residential, relying on nearby commercial centers for shopping and dining.

What are homes like in Summerview Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

The housing stock includes detached houses with rear-entry garages and attached townhomes. Across the broader South Jordan market, the median sale price is currently around $630,000. Specific prices in the village depend on the property's size and whether it's a newer build or an established home.

What restaurants and dining are in Summerview Village Daybreak?

The village itself doesn't host commercial dining within its residential streets. Residents travel to nearby commercial hubs within Daybreak and the greater South Jordan area for restaurants, coffee shops, and grocery stores.

What is the weather like in Summerview Village Daybreak?

The area experiences a four-season climate with hot, dry summers and snowy winters. Homes are built to handle these swings, typically featuring central air conditioning and robust heating systems.

What hospitals and medical care are near Summerview Village Daybreak?

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center on W Daybreak Parkway provides a 24-hour emergency room and clinical care. For major trauma, residents use the University of Utah Hospital or Intermountain Medical Center.

Which public schools serve Summerview Village in Daybreak?

The Jordan School District operates the public schools for this area, including Daybreak Elementary. Residents also have access to public charter schools like Early Light Academy.
