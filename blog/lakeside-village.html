Living in Lakeside Village, Daybreak, UT: Real Estate and Local Amenities for 2026

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

The median sale price in the broader South Jordan, UT market sits around $629,500, with homes spending roughly 50 days on the market. Daybreak is a major master-planned community within that market, and its Lake Village area - often called Lakeside Village and considered one of the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT - commands a premium for its direct waterfront access.

Buyers looking at homes for sale in Lakeside Village, Daybreak will find a mix of townhomes, condos, and single-family estates centered around Oquirrh Lake. Here's what housing costs in this specific sub-neighborhood, and what day-to-day life looks like once you're in.

What to Expect in Lakeside Village

The defining feature here is Oquirrh Lake - a 67-acre freshwater lake that anchors the entire community. Residents fish, paddle-board, and kayak on it throughout the warmer months, and the neighborhood layout is built around that access. Lake Village sits on the north bay, with water-view trails running through the area and an exclusive Waterside Club and beach available to residents.

This is Daybreak's premier waterfront district. It connects directly to the rest of the master-planned community, so you can reach the broader trail networks and commercial zones without ever getting in a car.

The Community Setting

Housing is built right into the lake's shoreline - custom lakefront estates, condos, and townhomes, many with private docks. Paths link residential streets directly to the water, making non-motorized transit genuinely practical rather than just a selling point. Buyers who want immediate lake access tend to focus their search here rather than on the broader Daybreak area.

How It Fits Into Daybreak

Daybreak encompasses thousands of homes across multiple distinct villages. Lake Village occupies a specific footprint on the north bay, with architecture and amenities geared specifically toward lakeside living.

Every resident pays the master Daybreak Community Association fee, which covers shared amenities across all villages. That integration matters - Lake Village residents have full access to the same parks and community centers as everyone else in Daybreak, not just the waterfront-specific amenities.

Real Estate and Home Prices in Lakeside Village

The broader South Jordan market currently carries about 459 homes in inventory, with an average sale-to-list ratio of nearly 99%. Within Daybreak itself, the median home price runs in the high $500,000s - recent community reports put it around $578,000.

Lake Village properties sell well above that Daybreak median. Condos, lakeside townhomes, and single-family homes in the "Waterside Manor" collection historically start in the $200,000s to $500,000s, but direct lakefront estates move easily into the $1 million range. With South Jordan homes taking about 50 days to sell on average, you have some room to evaluate your options - though direct waterfront estates are rarer and tend to draw faster offers when they do hit the market.

Home Types and Architecture

The housing stock in Lake Village runs from attached condos and townhomes up to large detached single-family homes, with the custom estates sitting directly on the lake representing the top tier. Many of these properties are built with outdoor living spaces designed to maximize water views. Daybreak is a relatively new development compared to older parts of Salt Lake County, so buyers should expect modern construction throughout.

HOA Fees and Community Rules

The master Daybreak Community Association fee is $144.50 per month for 2026 - mandatory for every property in the community, no exceptions.

If you're buying a townhome or condo in Lake Village, budget for sub-association fees on top of that. Those secondary HOAs can push your total monthly dues to $400, or over $600 in some buildings, depending on the specific property and what exterior maintenance the sub-association covers. That's not a detail to skim over when you're running the numbers.

Dining, Commuting, and Daily Life

Main roads in and around Daybreak include South Jordan Parkway, Daybreak Parkway, Mountain View Corridor, and 11400 South. These connect Lake Village to the rest of the Salt Lake Valley.

Commuters generally rely on the Mountain View Corridor for north-south travel toward Salt Lake City or the tech hubs in Lehi. Within the community itself, Daybreak's trail system makes it practical to get around by golf cart or bicycle - which, depending on where you work, might mean you're not driving at all during the week. Commercial zones are integrated directly into the neighborhood design, so daily errands don't require leaving the community.

Local Restaurants and Dining

Right on the water in the Northshore area, LakeBreak is the standout - a new upscale-casual restaurant on the shores of Oquirrh Lake, with a menu focused on seafood, burgers, and weekend brunch.

In Daybreak's SoDa Row district, a short distance away, you've got more everyday options: The Break Sports Grill, Moena Cafe, Nomad Eatery, Karuwaa Nepali & Indian Cuisine, and Cafe Trang Bistro.

Getting Around South Jordan

South Jordan Parkway runs east-west and connects Daybreak to Interstate 15, which makes reaching major employment centers manageable. The local infrastructure is designed to reduce vehicle traffic on residential streets, which works well in practice - but which side of Oquirrh Lake you live on does affect your access to the main thoroughfares, so it's worth paying attention to that when you're narrowing down specific streets.

Schools and Weather in Lakeside Village

Daybreak, including Lake Village, falls within the Jordan School District. Approximately 96% of Daybreak homes are zoned for Herriman High School. Local schools serving the community include Eastlake Elementary - which sits near Lake Village - along with Aspen/Daybreak Elementary and Copper Mountain Middle School. Attendance zones can shift based on enrollment, so confirm current boundaries directly with the Jordan School District before assuming any specific assignment.

South Jordan gets four distinct seasons. Summers are hot and dry, winters bring real snowfall, and Oquirrh Lake freezes over during the coldest months.

Local Climate and Seasons

Summer temperatures regularly exceed 90 degrees, which is the main reason the lake sees such heavy use for paddle-boarding and kayaking. Low humidity keeps the heat more bearable than you'd find in coastal areas.

Winter is genuine - snow in the Salt Lake Valley, cold temperatures, and driving conditions on the Mountain View Corridor that require attention from December through February. The lake as a summer amenity is real, but so is the winter.

Finding a Home in Lakeside Village

With roughly 3.5 months of housing supply currently available in South Jordan, the market is reasonably balanced for buyers right now. That said, about 13.6% of homes have recently sold above list price, so multiple-offer situations still happen - particularly on premium properties.

Direct lakefront estates and condos with private docks don't turn over frequently. You'll need to monitor inventory closely rather than waiting for a large selection to appear at once.

Before making an offer on anything here, read the HOA documents carefully. Understanding exactly how the master association dues and any sub-association fees break down isn't optional - it directly affects your monthly carrying costs.

Renting Versus Buying

Some buyers rent in Daybreak first to test the commute and the community before committing to a purchase. Rental homes in Lake Village do come to market occasionally, though less often than properties for sale. Leasing still gets you access to community amenities, including the Waterside Club - you just need to follow the Daybreak Community Association's rules on lake use and parking, same as any owner would.

Working With a Local Agent

An agent who knows Daybreak specifically can walk you through the pricing differences between Lake Village and the surrounding neighborhoods, and flag which properties carry sub-association fees before you're deep into a transaction. Ask them to pull recent sales data specifically for waterfront homes in South Jordan - the broader city median won't give you an accurate read on what a lakefront estate here is worth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lakeside Village in Daybreak known for?

Lakeside Village, formally known as Lake Village, is defined by its position on the north bay of Oquirrh Lake. The area features custom lakefront estates, water-view trails, and the exclusive Waterside Club, offering direct access to the 67-acre freshwater lake for kayaking and paddle-boarding.

What are homes like in Lakeside Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

The housing stock includes attached condos, townhomes, and large single-family estates. While the broader Daybreak median sits around $578,000, Lake Village properties run higher, with condos and townhomes starting in the $200,000s to $500,000s and direct lakefront estates exceeding $1 million.

Are short-term rentals allowed in Lakeside Village Daybreak?

Properties in this area are governed by the Daybreak Community Association, which enforces specific leasing and property use rules. Buyers interested in rental income should review the current HOA guidelines for minimum lease terms, as master-planned communities typically restrict short-term vacation rentals.

What restaurants and dining are in Lakeside Village Daybreak?

LakeBreak is the primary dining option directly on Oquirrh Lake, serving seafood and burgers in the Northshore area. A short distance away in SoDa Row, residents can access The Break Sports Grill, Moena Cafe, Nomad Eatery, Karuwaa Nepali & Indian Cuisine, and Cafe Trang Bistro.

What schools are assigned to the Lakeside Village neighborhood in Daybreak?

The neighborhood is part of the Jordan School District. Local students typically attend Eastlake Elementary, Aspen/Daybreak Elementary, Copper Mountain Middle School, and Herriman High School.

What is the weather like in Lakeside Village Daybreak?

South Jordan experiences four distinct seasons. Summers are hot and dry, which is ideal for using Oquirrh Lake, while winters bring cold temperatures and measurable snowfall to the Salt Lake Valley.
