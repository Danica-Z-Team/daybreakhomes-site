2026 Housing Market Trends in Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

The housing market in Daybreak, UT tracks closely with South Jordan's broader numbers - and right now, the median sale price sits at about $625,160 as of mid-2026. If you're buying a home in Daybreak, UT or selling in this master-planned community, you're working with roughly 3.7 months of inventory, which puts you somewhere in the middle of the road between a buyer's and seller's market. That's actually a reasonable place to be.

The numbers I'll walk you through - median prices, days on market, price per square foot - aren't just trivia. They're the difference between making a confident offer and second-guessing yourself at the worst possible moment.

Overview of the Daybreak Real Estate Market

In the South Jordan area that encompasses Daybreak, roughly 369 homes sold in recent months, spread across single-family houses and townhomes. There are about 447 active listings in the local market right now, which means buyers have real options - more than they've had in previous years. Homes are selling for roughly 99.1% of their list price on average, so sellers who price accurately are closing deals without handing out significant concessions.

Buyer or Seller Market Conditions

At 3.7 months of supply, this market doesn't belong firmly to either side. Under three months and you're in strong seller's territory; above six and buyers are clearly in control. Daybreak's Compete Score of 50 out of 100 tells the same story - there's room to negotiate, but a well-priced home will still draw multiple offers. Don't assume you can lowball your way through this market.

Comparing Daybreak to the Broader Area

Daybreak functions as its own master-planned community inside South Jordan, UT, and that distinction matters for pricing. The broader South Jordan median comes in at approximately $625,160, but within the 84009 ZIP code you'll often see something different, depending on property type. Condos and townhomes in Daybreak regularly offer a lower entry point than the larger single-family homes elsewhere in the city.

Current Home Prices in Daybreak, UT

The median sale price specifically within Daybreak runs around $563,000, and average house prices in the community hover near $545,000 - though that figure shifts month to month depending on the mix of condos and detached homes that actually closed. Think of these as your baseline, not a guarantee.

Pricing spreads wide here based on style and size. A townhome and a newly built detached single-family house with a yard are not the same conversation. Before you make any moves, pull recent comparable sales in the specific village you're targeting.

Median and Average Sale Prices

The broader South Jordan market's median of about $625,160 sits noticeably above Daybreak's localized median of $563,000 - and that gap comes down to the higher density of townhomes and condos in the community. Year-over-year, median sale prices in the broader region have decreased by roughly 3.7%. A softening, not a collapse.

Price Per Square Foot

The median sale price per square foot in Daybreak is approximately $240, up nearly 3.9% from last year. So even as overall medians have eased, buyers are still paying more per square foot than they were twelve months ago. Some active listings are asking closer to $253 per square foot, which is worth keeping in mind when you're evaluating whether an asking price makes sense.

Recent Market Trends and Inventory

As of mid-2026, buyers in the South Jordan area have about 447 active listings to work through. Within Daybreak specifically, active inventory typically runs between 237 and 374 depending on the week and the filters you're using. That range matters - you might have far more or far fewer options depending on exactly what you're looking for.

How long homes sit before going under contract tells you a lot about actual demand. Homes priced right and move-in ready tend to go quickly. Ones that linger usually need a price cut before they close.

Days on Market and Supply

The median days on market for the broader South Jordan area is 45 days. Inside Daybreak, that average stretches to about 54 days. For buyers, that pacing is genuinely useful - you have time to get inspections done and line up financing without someone else making a same-day offer over your head.

Are Home Prices Dropping?

Yes, modestly. The broader region saw a 3.7% dip in median sale prices year-over-year, and Daybreak-specific data shows a 4.6% decrease over a recent three-month period. But homes are still closing at 99.1% of list price, which tells you this is a correction, not a freefall. Sellers still have pricing power if they're realistic.

Market Outlook and Timing

About 15.8% of homes in the local market are still selling above list price. That's not nothing - it means the right property, priced well, still draws competitive offers. The market has pockets of real demand even as inventory has steadied.

A crash scenario requires massive oversupply and cratering demand. At 3.7 months of supply, neither of those conditions exists here. What you're watching is a normalization after a stretch of unusually fast appreciation - uncomfortable for some sellers, but not alarming.

Expectations for the Rest of the Year

Inventory should stay relatively stable through the back half of 2026. Sellers will need to price in line with recent comps - testing the market with an inflated number is more likely to produce a price reduction than a bidding war. Buyers who keep an eye on homes that have been sitting longer than 45 days may find the most room to negotiate.

Deciding When to Buy

Whether to buy in Daybreak, UT comes down to your financial readiness, not your ability to call the bottom of the market. Prices have softened from their peak, which gives you some negotiating room right now. Waiting for further drops is a gamble - if inventory tightens again, that window closes.

Cost of Living and Income Requirements

The median household income in Daybreak is approximately $103,307 - about 32% higher than the Utah state average. For the broader city of South Jordan, that figure rises to roughly $134,047.

Those numbers reflect what it actually costs to live in a community like this. Beyond the mortgage, you're looking at HOA dues, property taxes, and utilities. Run the full math before you commit to a price point.

Household Income Benchmarks

A household earning around $103,000 annually fits the typical financial profile of a Daybreak resident. If you're targeting the $563,000 median price, you'll need a solid down payment and a debt-to-income ratio your lender can work with. The standard benchmark lenders use is keeping housing costs below 28% of gross monthly income.

Assessing Overall Affordability

The mortgage is only part of the picture. Daybreak's amenities come with HOA fees that vary by community and property type. Make sure your lender and your budget account for the full monthly obligation - not just principal and interest.

Practical Advice for Buyers and Sellers

Homes in this market close at about 99.1% of asking price. For sellers, that means accurate initial pricing beats the strategy of listing high and waiting - a correctly priced home is more likely to find a buyer within that 45-day median window than one that sits and eventually requires a cut.

Buyers, get your pre-approval sorted before you start touring. When you find the right place, you don't want to lose it while you're still figuring out what a lender will give you.

Timing Your Sale

Utah sees real estate activity year-round, but winter months tend to bring fewer active buyers and thinner inventory. Sellers who list in spring or early summer are generally working with a larger pool of buyers, which can translate to faster movement. That said, less competition in winter isn't nothing either - it depends on your specific situation.

The 3-3-3 Rule Explained

You'll hear real estate professionals mention the 3-3-3 rule when talking about settling into a new home. The idea is simple: three days to get past the initial shock of moving, three weeks to establish basic routines, and three months to genuinely feel at home in a new community. It's a reasonable framework for managing your expectations after closing - especially in a new development where everything is still unfamiliar.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much are the average HOA fees in Daybreak, UT, and what community amenities do they cover?

It depends on the specific neighborhood and property type within Daybreak. Townhomes and condos typically carry different fee structures than single-family homes. Before you make an offer, request the HOA documents for that property and read them - you need to know exactly what's included and what it costs.

Is it more expensive to buy a house in Daybreak compared to the rest of South Jordan?

No. Recent data shows a median sale price of roughly $563,000 in Daybreak, compared to about $625,160 for the South Jordan area as a whole. That gap comes largely from the high concentration of condos and townhomes within the Daybreak neighborhood.

What happens if I want to rent out my Daybreak home - does the community have rental restrictions?

It depends on the HOA rules for your specific village or building. Many planned communities cap the percentage of homes that can be rented at any one time. If you're buying with investment in mind, request and read the governing documents before you close - not after.

How does the process of buying a new construction home in Daybreak differ from purchasing an existing resale?

New construction means you're buying directly from a builder, signing a builder-specific contract, and potentially waiting several months for the home to be finished. Resale purchases work on a more familiar timeline - typically a standard 30-day to 45-day closing once you're under contract.

How long does it typically take to sell a house in the current Daybreak market?

Homes in Daybreak average about 54 days on the market before selling. The broader South Jordan area comes in a bit faster at a median of 45 days. Accurate pricing is the biggest factor - homes that need a price reduction to find a buyer take longer than those that were priced right from the start.

Are there specific 55+ active adult or income-restricted neighborhoods within the Daybreak community?

It depends on the specific village. Your best move is to check current local listings and the community's official guidelines to see what specialized housing options are available. A local agent who knows the community well can point you toward the right villages without you having to sort through all of it yourself.
