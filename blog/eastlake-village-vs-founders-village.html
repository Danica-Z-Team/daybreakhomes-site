Eastlake Village vs Founders Village: Prices and Commutes

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

South Jordan's median sale price is sitting around $630,000 right now, with homes spending roughly 50 days on the market before going under contract. Daybreak is a big reason why South Jordan draws as much attention as it does - it's a master-planned community with several distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range and personality.

If you're narrowing your search to Daybreak, Eastlake Village and Founders Village are probably the two names you keep coming back to. They're different in ways that matter: architectural focus, position on Oquirrh Lake, and the types of properties you'll find for sale.

Comparing the Real Estate Markets in Eastlake and Founders

South Jordan has around 459 active homes for sale right now, representing about 3.5 months of supply. Daybreak accounts for a large share of that inventory - single-family homes, townhomes, and condos all mixed together.

The broader Daybreak community trends newer, with a median year built around 2020, a median lot size near 0.07 acres, and a median home size of about 2,575 square feet. Eastlake and Founders are older phases, which means something you won't find in the newest sections of the community: mature landscaping.

Eastlake Village Home Styles and Prices

Eastlake opened in 2006 as Daybreak's second phase, built along the east shore of Oquirrh Lake. Six different builders worked here, which shows - you get a wider range of architectural styles than almost anywhere else in the community, along with some of the largest homes in Daybreak.

Prices average around $651,000, with an average price per square foot of $223. The range is genuinely wide: a condo can come in around $309,000, while a large custom home on the lakefront can push past $2.2 million.

Founders Village Home Styles and Prices

Founders Village is where Daybreak started - the original phase, built along the lake's south side. The architecture takes its cues from Salt Lake City's Avenues neighborhood, so you're looking at classical styles, mature trees, and established sidewalks.

The median sale price in Founders Village runs approximately $525,000. The overall price range is similar to Eastlake's in terms of span - from around $350,000 up to $2 million or more for the largest properties on the water.

Commuting and Highway Access Around Daybreak

The main ways in and out of Daybreak are 10600 South and 11400 South, both with direct I-15 offramps. The Mountain View Corridor (SR-85) cuts through the center of the community and connects south toward Utah County and north toward Salt Lake City.

Bangerter Highway (SR-154) is close by as well, giving you another north-south option. Figure on about a half-hour drive to downtown Salt Lake City and roughly the same to the Silicon Slopes tech corridor in northern Utah County. If you'd rather take the train, TRAX light rail runs about 40 minutes into downtown Salt Lake City.

Driving from Eastlake Village

Eastlake uses the same primary Daybreak exits as the rest of the community. Most commuters leaving this neighborhood will use 11400 South or the Mountain View Corridor, depending on where they're headed.

Being on the east side of Oquirrh Lake puts Eastlake residents at a slight advantage when heading to Bangerter Highway or I-15 compared to neighborhoods further west.

Driving from Founders Village

Founders Village sits on the south side of the lake, right near 11400 South. That gives you a straightforward shot east to I-15 or west toward the Mountain View Corridor.

Drive times from Founders Village are essentially the same as the rest of Daybreak. That said, test your commute during actual rush hours before you buy - the established road network handles local traffic, but there's no substitute for knowing what your mornings will look like.

Community Amenities and HOA Fees

Daybreak HOA dues generally fall between $90 and $180 per month, depending on which village you're in and what type of property you're buying. Those fees cover the trails, pools, parks, and Oquirrh Lake maintenance that the whole community shares.

Some sections also roll exterior landscape maintenance into the dues. One thing buyers on condos and townhomes often miss: you'll pay a sub-association fee on top of the master HOA dues, not instead of it.

Eastlake Village Features

Eastlake Village has its own Eastlake Swimming Pool, two linear parks, and Eastlake Elementary. Streets like Lake Bridge Drive put you right at the water's edge. From there, SoDa Row and the paved walking trails along the full length of the lake are easy to reach.

Founders Village Features

Founders Village is anchored by its community center and Founders Park - the latter functions as the neighborhood's defining landmark. The mature tree canopy along the sidewalks gives it noticeably more shade than you'll find in the newer phases.

Because it sits on the south side of the lake, Founders residents connect easily to the broader Daybreak trail system and have the same lake access as the rest of the community.

Researching Schools and Local Data

Daybreak sits within South Jordan, Utah, under South Jordan City's governance, inside Salt Lake County. Both Eastlake and Founders fall within the Jordan School District, which covers South Jordan and other southwestern Salt Lake County communities.

For official school boundary maps and performance data, go directly to the Jordan School District website - that's where the authoritative information lives. On the public safety side, Daybreak is served by the South Jordan City Police Department. Their non-emergency line is (801) 840-4000 if you want to request neighborhood-specific statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is more expensive, Eastlake Village or Founders Village?

Eastlake Village runs higher. The average price there is around $651,000, while Founders Village's median sale price is closer to $525,000. Both neighborhoods have luxury waterfront homes that can exceed $2 million.

What types of homes are available in Eastlake Village vs Founders Village?

Both offer single-family homes, townhomes, and condos. Founders Village is known for its classical architecture inspired by Salt Lake City's Avenues neighborhood. Eastlake has a wider variety of styles, a product of having six different builders work in the community.

How do HOA fees compare between Eastlake Village and Founders Village in Daybreak?

Both villages fall within the standard Daybreak range of $90 to $180 per month. Buy a townhome or condo in either village and you'll pay an additional sub-association fee on top of that.

Which Daybreak neighborhood offers a faster commute to Salt Lake City, Eastlake or Founders Village?

They're essentially the same. Both neighborhoods have access to 10600 South, 11400 South, and the Mountain View Corridor, putting downtown Salt Lake City about 30 minutes away.

Do residents of Founders Village have the same access to Oquirrh Lake and community amenities as Eastlake Village?

Yes. All Daybreak residents share access to the lake, trails, pools, and parks. Founders Village is on the south side of the lake; Eastlake is on the east shore.

Are Eastlake Village and Founders Village zoned for the same public schools in Daybreak?

Both villages are part of the Jordan School District. Specific school assignments can vary by street, so check the district's boundary map for your address before assuming.

What is a common pitfall buyers face when choosing between the older Founders Village and Eastlake Village?

The sub-association fee on attached housing catches people off guard more than almost anything else. If you're looking at a condo or townhome in either village, that fee stacks on top of the main Daybreak community dues - it's not a replacement for it.
