Living in Blossom Village, Daybreak, UT: Homes, Cost, and Local Guide

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

The South Jordan, UT real estate market currently sees median home prices around $630,000. Within that market sits Daybreak - a large master-planned community made up of distinct sub-areas, one of which is Blossom Village. Many buyers consider this one of the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT.

Buyers looking at homes for sale in Blossom Village Daybreak will find a mix of property styles woven into the broader community's trail and park system. The housing inventory, daily life, and commute clarify what you pay for when you buy here.

With roughly 459 active listings across the broader South Jordan area and homes spending an average of 50 days on the market, you have real time to evaluate your options. This guide covers property types, local healthcare, and the assigned school district for this specific section of Daybreak.

What to Expect in Blossom Village

Blossom Village is a distinct residential section inside the larger Daybreak master-planned community in South Jordan, UT. The whole place is built around walkability - pedestrian paths connect residential streets to shared green spaces, and the housing layout is deliberately designed to put you close to the community's broader trail network without a car trip in between.

Homes here fall under the Daybreak homeowners association, which handles upkeep of the shared amenities and enforces architectural guidelines. That structure matters when you're budgeting, and it matters when you're deciding what you can and can't do with the exterior of your home.

The neighborhood integrates directly into the rest of the development. Residents can access the larger man-made lake, commercial centers, and recreational facilities that serve the entire Daybreak population - it's not a pocket that feels cut off from everything else.

Real Estate and Housing Costs

Recent market data for the South Jordan area shows a median sale price of roughly $630,000, with homes typically selling for about 98.9% of their list price. Blossom Village Daybreak real estate largely mirrors those broader trends, offering a mix of single-family homes and attached properties.

The housing stock runs from established homes to properties with modern layouts designed to make the most of the lot. One line item buyers sometimes underestimate: Daybreak community HOA fees. Those dues cover the extensive landscaping and shared infrastructure, so they're not optional and they're not small.

At about 50 days on the market, you're not walking into a war. You have time to inspect properties and read through the HOA documents carefully. That said, sellers should price close to recent comparables - buyers here are paying very close to asking, which means the room for negotiation is narrower than it looks.

Daily Life, Dining, and Getting Around

Daybreak was designed so that routine errands don't require getting on a highway. The community has dedicated retail spaces where residents grab coffee, eat at neighborhood eateries, or pick up groceries - many people walk or bike to those spots from Blossom Village. For larger shopping trips or specialized retail, the broader South Jordan commercial corridors are a short drive out.

Getting out of the neighborhood for work means connecting to the arterial roads that border South Jordan. The community layout funnels traffic toward those thoroughfares, which then give you straightforward access to the rest of the Salt Lake Valley and regional transit.

Schools, Healthcare, and Weather

The Jordan School District serves residents here. The specific public schools assigned to the area are Daybreak School, Mountain Creek Middle School, and Herriman High.

For medical care, the University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center sits at 5126 W. Daybreak Parkway, South Jordan, UT 84009. It has a 24/7 emergency department, so you're not driving across the valley for urgent care.

Weather follows the standard four-season pattern of the Salt Lake Valley - hot, dry summers that make the community lake genuinely useful, and snowy winters that mean your home maintenance budget needs to account for snow removal and winterization.

Finding a Home in the Neighborhood

The broader South Jordan market saw 398 homes sold over a single reporting period, which tells you there's real, steady movement in the local supply. Inventory in this specific village shifts throughout the year, so if you're serious, you'll want to watch the Daybreak listings consistently rather than checking in occasionally.

Renting first is a legitimate option if you want to live the commute and understand the neighborhood layout before you commit to a purchase. If you're ready to buy, get your financing in order early - and make sure whoever is helping you through the process knows the Daybreak subdivisions, because the specific assessments and utility setups here aren't the same as a standard South Jordan resale.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Blossom Village in Daybreak known for?

Blossom Village is known as a pedestrian-focused residential section within the Daybreak master-planned community. It features a mix of housing types connected by trails and shared green spaces that link directly into the broader South Jordan area.

You'll find a mix of single-family homes and attached properties, all governed by the community HOA. Prices vary within the village, but the median sale price for the broader South Jordan area currently sits around $630,000.

What is the weather like in Blossom Village Daybreak?

What hospitals and medical care are near Blossom Village Daybreak?

What are the assigned public schools for residents of Blossom Village in Daybreak?

The Jordan School District serves the neighborhood. Assigned public schools are Daybreak School, Mountain Creek Middle School, and Herriman High.
