Lakeside Village vs. Lake Village in Daybreak, UT: Same Area?

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

South Jordan's broader real estate market recently saw a median sale price around $629,583, but homes on the water in Daybreak often command a premium. These waterfront communities are widely considered some of the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT. Buyers looking for water views frequently ask about the differences between Lakeside Village and Lake Village.

Both names refer to the same prime real estate built around Oquirrh Lake in Salt Lake County. Understanding how these areas overlap - and what they offer - goes a long way toward cutting through the confusion for buyers who've been Googling both terms and wondering if they're missing something.

Property Types and Market Prices

Active listings in Lake Village currently show an average price of roughly $734,000, with luxury waterfront homes reaching up to $2.4 million. The broader South Jordan market has about 459 homes in inventory and averages 50 days on the market, though lake-oriented properties here tend to follow their own demand curves.

When buyers search for Lakeside Village, they're looking at an older phase name from a 2012 development rollout on the same lake peninsula. That phase is now generally marketed and subsumed under the broader Lake Village or Eastlake umbrellas. The housing stock is essentially the same either way.

Architectural Styles Along the Water

Homes in this section of Daybreak lean toward front-porch traditional and craftsman designs. Builders incorporated rear or tucked garages to maximize curb appeal and keep the streets genuinely walkable - something you'll notice the moment you turn onto one of these blocks.

Floor plans in Lake Village are specifically oriented to take advantage of the Oquirrh Mountains and the water. Properties on the west shore and the east peninsula often feature usable yard space that blends directly into the surrounding trails.

Current Price Ranges and Inventory

Entry-level homes in the Lake Village area start around $480,000, which typically buys a smaller footprint or townhome layout. The average price per square foot sits near $251 across the active inventory.

There are no separate, current market statistics published exclusively for the Lakeside Village name. Buyers should expect to pay Lake Village prices for any property located on that central peninsula - because that's exactly what it is.

Commuting and Local Transit Options

The drive from Daybreak to downtown Salt Lake City covers about 20.4 miles, and most commuters make that trip in roughly 40 minutes via Bangerter Highway (SR-154) or Interstate 15. Because Lakeside Village and Lake Village occupy the same general area around Oquirrh Lake, residents share identical commute times. Getting to the main arteries requires just a short drive through the community's internal streets.

Highway Access

Bangerter Highway runs directly along Daybreak's eastern edge and provides the fastest route north toward the city. The Mountain View Corridor (SR-85) cuts through the center of the wider Daybreak community as an alternative north-south arterial.

From the lake peninsula, drivers can reach either of these roads in just a few minutes. Traffic inside the neighborhood moves slowly by design - pedestrians and cyclists come first here.

Public Transportation Routes

Daybreak has its own TRAX Red Line stations for commuters who'd rather skip the highway altogether. Riding light rail into downtown Salt Lake City takes between 50 and 90 minutes depending on your exact stop and the time of day.

Residents near Oquirrh Lake can bike or walk to the nearest TRAX station without much trouble. For anyone who dreads winter highway driving, that's a genuinely useful option.

Oquirrh Lake Amenities and Association Rules

The Daybreak Community Association governs the entire residential development, so there's no separate HOA entity for Lakeside Village or Lake Village - both sections fall under the same master association rules.

The centerpiece of this area is Oquirrh Lake itself, surrounded by walking trails and boardwalks, with homes built specifically to maximize water access and the private facilities that serve the peninsula.

Shared Neighborhood Features

The lake's west shore and east peninsula offer direct water access, and some homes include private kayak or canoe sheds. Residents also have use of the private Waterside Club, which provides boat storage for non-motorized watercraft.

Beyond the water, the association maintains parks, community pools, and open green spaces throughout. The trail system connects lakefront properties to the rest of the Daybreak villages.

Association Dues and Coverage

Daybreak-wide HOA dues typically range from $90 to $180 per month, depending on the specific village and property type. Some local market reports note that certain homes or townhomes may see fees around $295 per month if exterior landscape maintenance is included.

These fees cover the shared amenities - the lake, trails, and community centers. Buyers should verify the exact monthly assessment for any specific property, since the older Lakeside Village phase and newer Lake Village homes all pay into this same system.

Making Your Decision in Daybreak

With South Jordan's overall median days on market at 50 days, you have a moderate window to evaluate your options. Waterfront properties in Daybreak, though, tend to attract immediate attention when they're priced right.

Choosing between Lakeside Village and Lake Village is ultimately a question of semantics, not a choice between two distinct neighborhoods. You're looking at the same geographic footprint, the same amenities, and the same community association. The decision that matters comes down to the specific lot, the floor plan, and what the water views look like from the living room window.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference in home prices between Lakeside Village and Lake Village in Daybreak?

There is no current price difference. Lakeside Village was an older phase name from 2012 that is now generally marketed as part of Lake Village. Active listings in the Lake Village area average around $734,000 today, with prices ranging from about $480,000 to $2.4 million depending on size and water proximity.

Do Lakeside Village and Lake Village have different HOA fees?

No. Both areas are governed by the Daybreak Community Association. Monthly dues typically run between $90 and $180, though some properties may see fees around $295 if exterior maintenance is included.

Which community has better access to Daybreak commuting routes, Lakeside Village or Lake Village?

They offer the exact same access, because they're located in the same area around Oquirrh Lake. Residents of both can reach Bangerter Highway on the eastern edge or the Mountain View Corridor running through the center of Daybreak.

Do residents of Lakeside Village and Lake Village get the same access to Oquirrh Lake and other Daybreak amenities?

Yes - identical access. Both enjoy the scenic walking trails, boardwalks, and the private Waterside Club for boat storage.

Are there different property types or architectural styles in Lakeside Village compared to Lake Village?

The architectural styles are consistent across the peninsula - mostly front-porch traditional and craftsman designs. Both phases were built to maximize water views and blend with the broader Daybreak aesthetic.

Which of these Daybreak, UT neighborhoods is better for finding new construction homes versus existing properties?

Because Lakeside Village was an early phase introduced around 2012, homes carrying that specific name will be existing properties. The broader Lake Village area may have newer builds, but both primarily consist of established homes on the lake peninsula.

What are the most common buyer trade-offs when choosing between Lakeside Village and Lake Village?

There aren't meaningful trade-offs between the two - they occupy the same geographic footprint and share the same HOA. The real decision comes down to individual property features, such as whether a specific home has a private kayak shed or direct lake views.
