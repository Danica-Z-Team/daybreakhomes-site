Pros and Cons of Living in Daybreak, UT: A 2026 Relocation Guide

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

The median home sale price in the South Jordan, UT area sits around $625,160 as of mid-2026. Within that market sits Daybreak - a massive master-planned community with distinct architecture and more trail mileage than most buyers expect. For anyone buying a home in Daybreak, the question isn't whether it's a well-built community. It is. The question is whether it's the right fit for you.

Relocating to this part of Salt Lake County means getting clear on the cost of living, your actual commute, and the property rules you'll be living under. This guide works through the real numbers on housing costs, crime statistics, and everyday utility access - the stuff the brochures gloss over.

The Quick Verdict on Living in Daybreak

Homes in the broader South Jordan area spend a median of 45 days on the market before selling, with about 3.7 months of housing supply across the local market. That's not a frenzied pace, but it's not slow either.

Whether this community works for you comes down to one honest question: do the lifestyle features justify the financial structure? For some buyers, absolutely. Others look at the same package and head to Herriman instead.

Pros to Consider

The community is built around accessibility and recreation. You've got miles of trails, a man-made lake, and community centers - all baked into the plan from day one. Housing ranges from townhomes to large single-family houses, so there's genuine variety across different budgets.

Potential Drawbacks

The cost of living index here is 106.9, putting it slightly above the national average. On top of that, HOA fees cover those extensive amenities - which means they're real and recurring, not optional. Inventory is tightly controlled, and the property rules can feel restrictive if you're used to making your own calls about your land.

Factors to Weigh

Commute times and natural hazard exposure deserve a hard look before you sign anything. Access to local shops is solid, but if you're driving outside of South Jordan every day, highway traffic becomes part of your routine.

Location and Proximity Details

Daybreak sits entirely within the city limits of South Jordan, on the western side of the Salt Lake Valley, in Salt Lake County. It's part of the broader Salt Lake City metropolitan region.

Downtown Salt Lake City is roughly 20 to 30 minutes away depending on traffic - not a punishing drive, but it's a drive.

County and Commute Access

Salt Lake County puts you within reach of major employment hubs and regional transit networks. Most drivers use the Mountain View Corridor or Bangerter Highway to connect to Interstate 15 for north-south travel.

Population and Size

The numbers here shift depending on where you draw the lines. Point2Homes reports 6,604 residents in the Daybreak neighborhood proper based on Census data, while broader ZIP code definitions push that number over 40,000. Both figures are accurate - they're just measuring different things.

Crime Statistics and Data

Law enforcement for Daybreak runs through the South Jordan Police Department. A new Public Safety Building - Station 64 - with a police substation recently opened directly in Downtown Daybreak to improve response times on the west side of the city.

Look at the published figures, not the neighborhood Facebook groups.

Reported Crime Rates

CrimeGrade reports an overall crime rate of 13.43 incidents per 1,000 residents, placing Daybreak in the 98th percentile for safety from murder and the 99th for assault. This means the community is safer than the vast majority of U.S. neighborhoods in these categories.

Where to Find Official Records

Go directly to the South Jordan Police Department or the Salt Lake County Sheriff's Office for the most accurate information. The FBI's Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) program data for South Jordan also gives you historical trends you can actually track over time.

Researching Specific Streets

Local police departments often maintain public crime mapping tools that break down recent incident reports by block or intersection. If you're seriously considering a specific section of the community, pull those logs and see what type of calls are coming from that area. It takes twenty minutes and tells you a lot.

Cost of Living and Housing Prices

The median sale price for homes in the South Jordan area is roughly $625,160, with sellers receiving about 99.1% of their list price on average. Within Daybreak specifically, average home prices have hovered in the mid-$500,000s - Redfin reported an average of $545,000 in a recent month. That gap reflects the mix of townhomes versus single-family houses selling in any given window.

Everyday Expenses

The overall cost of living index in Daybreak is 106.9. Groceries, utilities, transportation - you'll pay slightly more for all of it than the average U.S. consumer. Not dramatically more, but it adds up.

Home Prices and Real Estate Trends

With an average of 45 days on the market and about 15.8% of homes selling above list price, this isn't a market where you can afford to be indecisive. Come prepared.

Income Needed to Buy

Based on a median list price in the high $500,000s and a 25% down payment, Movoto estimates you'd need a total yearly income of about $104,000 to comfortably carry the mortgage, taxes, and insurance. That's a reasonable baseline for planning purposes.

Community Facts and Figures

Per U.S. Census Bureau data compiled by Point2Homes, Daybreak has a median household income of $126,968 - up 3.2% year-over-year. Average household income estimates sit somewhat higher at $158,538.

The community's demographic profile reflects its housing mix and its location in the Salt Lake Valley.

Age and Population Estimates

Median age figures vary depending on how boundaries are drawn. Point2Homes reports a median age of 39.1 for the South Jordan portion of Daybreak, while other boundary definitions suggest a younger median age somewhere around 30 to 33.

Median Household Income Trends

Income varies considerably by age group. Householders between 45 and 64 show a median income of $171,623. For householders under 25, that figure drops to $85,329. If you're buying as a younger household, it's worth running those numbers carefully against the purchase price and HOA obligations.

Civic Amenities

The community is designed around shared spaces - community gardens, extensive walking paths, and centralized gathering spots that host local events throughout the year. If you want neighbors you'll actually see, the infrastructure supports that.

Climate and Recreation

Daybreak carries a moderate flood risk overall, with less than 1% of properties projected to face severe flooding risk over the next 30 years. In practical terms, that translates to about 27 properties in the community facing notable flood exposure.

If you're coming from outside Utah, the high desert climate takes some adjusting to. Summers are hot and dry. Winters bring snow, and that means you need reliable road clearing and a home that can handle real heating demands.

Natural Hazard Exposure

Flooding isn't the only thing to review. The Salt Lake Valley sits near the Wasatch Fault, so earthquake preparedness is a legitimate consideration for anyone buying here. Verify whether your specific property requires additional insurance coverage for seismic activity - don't assume the standard policy covers it.

Parks and Recreation

Oquirrh Lake is the centerpiece of the master plan, open for non-motorized boating and paddleboarding. The surrounding parks include open green space, playgrounds, and sports courts woven directly into the residential blocks. It's a well-thought-out layout for people who actually want to use outdoor space.

Comparing Daybreak to Salt Lake City

Downtown Salt Lake City is roughly 20 to 30 minutes from South Jordan. The choice between the two isn't really about which is better - it's about what your daily life actually looks like.

Salt Lake City's urban core gives you high-density living and immediate access to major commercial centers. Daybreak gives you a planned suburban environment with more space between properties and newer construction throughout.

Tradeoffs to Consider

Salt Lake City has older, established neighborhoods - varied architectural styles, mature trees, blocks that weren't designed by a committee. Daybreak offers newer construction, uniform community standards, and modern energy-efficient building practices. Neither is objectively superior. They're just different.

Housing Types and Commute Times

If you're working downtown, you're committing to a daily highway commute from Daybreak - plan accordingly. If you're in the Silicon Slopes tech corridor in Lehi, South Jordan often works as a convenient middle ground with a more manageable drive in either direction.

Specialized Housing Options

The Daybreak master plan includes specific zoning for facility types beyond standard single-family homes, including developments geared toward specialized care and transitional housing. These facilities share the same infrastructure and trail access as the rest of the community.

Buyers and families looking for specific accommodations have several options integrated into the broader neighborhood.

Senior and Assisted Living Facilities

The area includes dedicated senior living and assisted living centers with tiered care levels. For families looking to keep older relatives nearby in South Jordan while ensuring they have access to medical and daily living support, that's a practical option worth knowing about.

Sober Living Accommodations

Sober living homes operate in and around the South Jordan area. These properties provide structured, substance-free environments for individuals transitioning out of rehabilitation programs.

Local Amenities and Services

Daybreak is served by the local South Jordan-area public school system. A local real estate source notes that 96% of Daybreak homes feed into Herriman High School and Copper Mountain Middle School. Elementary zoning is split across several campuses - Aspen Elementary, Daybreak Elementary, and Eastlake Elementary. Always verify the specific boundary maps with the district before you buy. School zones shift, and you don't want a surprise after closing.

Public Schools and Parks

The schools are built into the community layout, so many students can walk or bike to class using the off-street trail network. The parks department maintains pocket parks and sports fields adjacent to those campuses.

Dining and Entertainment

SoDa Row gives residents local restaurants, cafes, and boutique shops without leaving the neighborhood. It's not a full urban commercial district, but it covers the basics and then some for a weekend morning or a weeknight dinner.

Healthcare Access

The University of Utah Health operates a medical center directly within the community. Primary care, emergency services, specialist appointments - you don't have to drive into Salt Lake City for any of it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much are the HOA fees in Daybreak, UT, and what amenities do they actually cover?

It depends on the specific village and property type within the community. Fees generally cover access to Oquirrh Lake, the community centers, pools, and maintenance of the extensive trail network. Townhome owners typically pay additional fees for exterior building maintenance and landscaping.

Are the Daybreak HOA rules and restrictions really as strict as people say?

Yes. The community enforces detailed architectural and landscaping standards. Exterior modifications require a submitted plan, and the association actively monitors compliance to maintain the uniform look of the master-planned development. If you want to paint your door an unusual color or put up a basketball hoop, expect a process.

What is the Daybreak community enhancement fee buyers have to pay at closing?

Buyers are required to pay a one-time community enhancement fee at closing. It goes toward funding community events, local arts, and ongoing neighborhood improvements. Budget for it before you get to the closing table.

Is the daily commute from Daybreak to downtown Salt Lake City or Silicon Slopes manageable?

For most drivers, yes. Downtown Salt Lake City is roughly 20 to 30 minutes away via major highways, and the Silicon Slopes tech corridor in Lehi is also accessible for daily commuters. Traffic is the variable - commute times at 8 a.m. on a Monday are not the same as a Sunday afternoon test drive.

How does buying a home in Daybreak compare to living in neighboring areas like Herriman or standard South Jordan?

It comes down to master-planned amenities versus property autonomy. Daybreak offers a tightly structured environment with walkability and built-in recreation. Standard South Jordan or Herriman properties often feature larger lots and fewer HOA restrictions. One isn't better - they suit different buyers.

Do homes in the Daybreak community appreciate at the same rate as the rest of Salt Lake County?

Homes here generally follow the broader South Jordan market trends. With the local median sale price sitting around $625,160 and homes selling at 99.1% of list price, demand has been steady and comparable to the rest of the county.
