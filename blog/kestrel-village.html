What to Expect When Living in the Kestrel Village Area of Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

South Jordan's Daybreak community is organized into distinct villages, and Kestrel Rise Road runs through the middle of some of the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT. The median home price in the broader South Jordan area sits around $629,500, with homes spending roughly 50 days on the market before selling.

If you're shopping for homes in the Kestrel Village area of Daybreak, UT, you'll find a mix of townhomes, condominiums, and single-family houses. The street cuts directly through Founders Park Village, Heights Park Village, and Creekside - giving residents immediate access to community parks, local dining, and established transit routes.

Understanding the Kestrel Rise Road Area

Here's something buyers run into constantly: they search for a specific "Kestrel Village" subdivision and can't quite pin it down. That's because Kestrel Rise Road functions as a main corridor connecting multiple Daybreak neighborhoods - Founders Park Village, Heights Park Village, and Creekside - rather than a single named subdivision. The amenities are shared across all of them.

The layout is built around walkability and outdoor recreation. Founders Park, located at 11428 S Kestrel Rise Road, anchors this stretch - splash pool, basketball and volleyball courts, play equipment, and a community garden. Paved trail networks and sidewalks run parallel to the main roads, linking residential blocks directly to commercial areas and open spaces, so you're not reaching for your car keys every time you need something.

Real Estate and Home Prices in the Kestrel Area

South Jordan currently carries about 3.5 months of housing supply, with 459 active listings across the city. Within Daybreak itself, the median sale price recently hovered between $559,990 and $574,000 as of mid-2026.

The housing stock along the Kestrel Rise corridor varies widely in both size and price. Entry-level condominiums and townhomes generally start around $350,000, while large single-family homes - including properties near the community's water features - can exceed $1 million.

One thing worth understanding before you commit to a specific address: Kestrel Rise Road passes through different phases of Daybreak's development, which means properties belong to different sub-associations with varying monthly dues and exterior maintenance requirements. Review the HOA rules for the specific village you're considering. They're not all the same.

Dining, Shopping, and Daily Life

SoDa Row, Daybreak's primary dining and retail district, sits directly on Kestrel Rise Road between 11259 and 11274 S Kestrel Rise Road. You can walk to it from most of the corridor.

The restaurant mix covers a reasonable range - Porch Restaurant for New American and Southern comfort food, Cupbop for fast-casual Korean BBQ, and The Break Sports Grill and The Break Cafe for casual meals and morning coffee. It's not a massive dining scene, but it's genuinely walkable, which counts for a lot day-to-day.

For getting around, commuters can use nearby light rail stations or major thoroughfares like Mountain View Corridor and Bangerter Highway to reach downtown Salt Lake City or neighboring employment centers.

Schools, Healthcare, and Local Weather

The Kestrel Rise Road corridor falls within the Jordan School District. Students in this part of Daybreak typically attend Daybreak Elementary and Eastlake Elementary, both within reach on foot.

The district has also purchased sites near 10200 S and Kestrel Rise Road in Creekside Village for future school development. Attendance boundaries can shift when new facilities open, so verify current zones directly with the district rather than assuming what's true today holds tomorrow.

Medical Facilities and Climate

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center at 5126 W Daybreak Parkway is right within the Daybreak community. It's not a small urgent-care clinic - the facility includes a Huntsman Cancer Institute satellite office and a full-service, 24-hour emergency room equipped with AirMed flight transportation.

As for weather, the Kestrel Village area follows the standard Salt Lake Valley pattern: four distinct seasons, hot and dry summers, cold and snowy winters. If you're coming from somewhere with milder winters, budget for proper home insulation and plan on seasonal property maintenance.

Finding a Home Along Kestrel Rise Road

South Jordan properties average 50 days on the market, and sellers are currently receiving about 98.9% of their list price - a balanced environment where there's room to negotiate without expecting dramatic discounts.

Whether you want a low-maintenance townhome walking distance from SoDa Row or a larger single-family house near Founders Park, the corridor genuinely offers multiple entry points into Daybreak at different price levels. Because the road passes through several different villages and sub-associations, working with a local agent who knows the inventory across all of them will save you from a lot of back-and-forth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kestrel Village in Daybreak known for?

The Kestrel Village area is known as a central corridor in Daybreak anchored by Kestrel Rise Road. It features a mix of townhomes and single-family houses, along with direct access to Founders Park and the SoDa Row dining district.

What are homes like in Kestrel Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

Homes along the Kestrel Rise corridor range from condominiums starting around $350,000 to large single-family properties exceeding $1 million. The broader Daybreak community has a median sale price between $559,990 and $574,000.

What restaurants and dining are in Kestrel Village Daybreak?

The area features the SoDa Row dining district directly on Kestrel Rise Road. Local restaurants include Porch Restaurant for Southern comfort food, Cupbop Korean BBQ, The Break Sports Grill, and The Break Cafe.

What hospitals and medical care are near Kestrel Village Daybreak?

The primary facility is the University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center at 5126 W Daybreak Parkway. It provides a 24-hour emergency room, AirMed flight transportation, and a Huntsman Cancer Institute satellite clinic.

Which public schools are assigned to students living in Kestrel Village Daybreak?

Properties in this area are part of the Jordan School District. Nearby schools include Daybreak Elementary and Eastlake Elementary, and the district has purchased additional land in Creekside Village for future schools.
