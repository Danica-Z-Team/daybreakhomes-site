Living in Founders Village, Daybreak, UT: Housing, Costs, and Daily Life

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

Founders Village is the original neighborhood that launched the Daybreak master-planned community in South Jordan, UT. Today, it stands out as one of the best neighborhoods in Daybreak. Built on the south side of Oquirrh Lake, it established the architectural style and community layout that defines the broader area today.

If you're looking at homes for sale in Founders Village Daybreak, you're looking at established properties with immediate access to commercial hubs like SoDa Row. Knowing the housing costs, association rules, and everyday amenities upfront saves you from surprises later.

The Layout of Founders Village in Daybreak, UT

Founders Village sits on the south side of Oquirrh Lake, and as the first village built in Daybreak, its design draws direct inspiration from Salt Lake City's historic Avenues neighborhood. Walk the streets here and you'll notice the landscaping is noticeably more mature than what you see out on the newer construction edges of the master plan.

The central landmark is Founders Park at 11428 Kestrel Rise Road - a community garden, basketball and volleyball courts, a splash pool, and sensory play equipment, all within a short walk for most residents. The lake's southern trail network starts right there too.

Connecting to the Broader Community

Being part of the larger Daybreak association means you're not limited to what's in your immediate village. The trail systems and community centers spread across South Jordan are yours to use. Pedestrian paths link the residential blocks directly to commercial areas, which is a deliberate part of how this neighborhood was designed.

Because Founders Village was the first phase of development, it sits close to the community's main entry points. Getting out to the rest of the Salt Lake Valley for work or errands is straightforward from here.

Real Estate Prices and Market Trends

The median sale price across South Jordan recently hit roughly $629,500, with homes averaging 50 days on the market. Inside Daybreak specifically, that median drops to around $574,000. In Founders Village, local brokerages report a median sale price of approximately $525,000.

That's a meaningful step below the newer, higher-priced phases of the master plan, and it's one of the reasons buyers end up here after touring the whole community. South Jordan currently carries around 459 active listings, so you'll have reasonable inventory to work through. Sellers are getting close to their asking price - the sale-to-list ratio is running at about 98.9% - so don't expect much softness in negotiations.

Housing Stock and Property Types

Most of what you'll find here is single-family homes and townhouses built during the initial phases of Daybreak's development. Alley-loaded garages and front porches are the signature look, consistent with those Avenues-inspired architectural guidelines.

These are established homes, not new builds, which means you should go in with eyes open about early-2000s finishes that may need updating. Every property falls under the Daybreak Community Association, which sets exterior standards and manages the shared amenities.

Dining, Shopping, and Daily Routine

SoDa Row is right at the edge of Founders Village and handles most of the day-to-day dining without you needing to get in your car. You've got Cupbop Korean BBQ, Tio's Mexican Restaurant, Porch Restaurant for Southern comfort food, The Break Sports Bar & Grill, Maxwell's Pizza & Cheesesteaks, Ground To Earth, and Sweet & Cool all operating along that strip. Biscotts Bakery & Café is nearby in the Highland Park area if you want coffee and pastries in the morning. The adjacent Downtown Daybreak district has also picked up newer additions - Red Iguana and Nomad Eatery have both opened there.

Getting Around the Area

Wide sidewalks and dedicated paths connect the residential streets directly to SoDa Row, so a morning coffee or an evening out doesn't require driving. That's genuinely useful on a daily basis.

For larger grocery runs and big-box retail, you're heading out to the commercial centers along Mountain View Corridor or Bangerter Highway. That's the honest tradeoff - walkable for casual needs, car-dependent for the bigger hauls.

Local Schools, Healthcare, and Climate

Founders Village falls within the Jordan School District. School catchments can shift as new villages come online in Daybreak, so verify your exact school assignments directly with the Jordan School District before you close - don't rely on what a listing says.

On the healthcare side, the University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center is located inside Daybreak at 5126 W Daybreak Pkwy and includes a 24-hour emergency room. The main University of Utah Hospital, a Level I Trauma Center, is in Salt Lake City. CommonSpirit Holy Cross Hospital - Jordan Valley is about four miles away.

Weather Expectations

Founders Village Daybreak sits in Utah's dry, semi-arid climate zone with four real seasons. Summers run 70 to 95 degrees with occasional monsoonal thunderstorms late in the season. Winters get cold - lows in the teens and twenties are normal, and the Salt Lake Valley gets winter air inversions that can be rough for people sensitive to air quality.

The most temperate stretches are late April through mid-June and late August through mid-October. Plan around those windows if you're visiting before making a decision.

Short-Term Rentals and Investment Rules

This section matters a lot if you're thinking about this as an investment purchase. Short-term rentals - Airbnb, VRBO, anything like that - are completely prohibited throughout the entire Daybreak community. That's not a gray area; it's a firm association rule.

If you want to rent out your home at all, you must live in it as your primary residence for 12 consecutive months first. After that, any lease has to cover the entire property and run for a minimum of 30 consecutive days.

Long-Term Leasing Restrictions

The association also limits you to holding one rental property in the community at a time. That rule keeps the owner-occupancy ratio high and shuts down the idea of building a portfolio of rentals here.

Review the association's current bylaws during due diligence - not after you're under contract, during. Your lender will be looking at these restrictions too, particularly if you're financing an investment property.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Founders Village in Daybreak known for?

Founders Village is the original residential phase of the Daybreak master-planned community. Its architecture is inspired by Salt Lake City's Avenues neighborhood, and it offers direct access to Founders Park and the southern edge of Oquirrh Lake.

What are homes like in Founders Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

You'll mostly find established single-family homes and townhouses with alley-loaded garages and front porches. The median sale price in Founders Village is roughly $525,000, which sits below the broader Daybreak median of $574,000.

What restaurants and dining are in Founders Village Daybreak?

Most dining is concentrated along SoDa Row and includes Cupbop Korean BBQ, Tio's Mexican Restaurant, Porch Restaurant, Maxwell's Pizza & Cheesesteaks, The Break Sports Bar & Grill, and Ground To Earth.

What is the weather like in Founders Village Daybreak?

Expect a dry, semi-arid climate with summer highs between 70 and 95 degrees and cold winters with lows in the teens and twenties. The mildest weather falls in spring and early fall.

What hospitals and medical care are near Founders Village Daybreak?

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center is inside Daybreak at 5126 W Daybreak Pkwy and has a 24-hour ER. CommonSpirit Holy Cross Hospital - Jordan Valley is about four miles away, and the main University of Utah Hospital is in Salt Lake City.

Can I use my property in Founders Village Daybreak as a short-term rental or Airbnb?

No. Short-term rentals are prohibited throughout the entire Daybreak community. You must live in the property for 12 consecutive months before renting it out, and all leases must run for at least 30 consecutive days.

How much are the HOA fees in Founders Village, and what Daybreak amenities do they include?

Dues to the Daybreak Community Association apply to every home in Founders Village, though the exact amount varies by property type. Those fees cover maintenance of shared spaces including Founders Park, Oquirrh Lake, and the community's trail network.
