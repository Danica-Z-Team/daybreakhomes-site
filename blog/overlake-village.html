Real Estate Guide for Overlake Village in Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

South Jordan's master-planned community of Daybreak is one of the most active housing markets in the Salt Lake Valley. The median sale price across South Jordan sits around $629,583, and homes are spending roughly 50 days on the market before closing. Buyers come in with specific checklists to find the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT - particular enclaves, certain amenities, a commute that works.

When buyers search for Overlake Village Daybreak, UT, they're usually looking for Eastlake Village or homes near Overlook Park. Daybreak has distinct neighborhoods - Founders Park, Garden Park, Highland Park - but Overlake isn't an official village name in the community. What the Eastlake area does offer is exactly what those buyers are after: established homes, clear mountain views, and proximity to the water.

The Layout of Daybreak and Eastlake Village

Daybreak is divided into distinct villages, each built around specific parks, water features, or commercial centers. The closest match to the Overlake name is Overlook Park, located at 10749 S Fern Ridge Dr in Eastlake Village. That park sits on the east end of the community and delivers clear sunrise and sunset views across the valley.

Searching for homes near that park puts you in one of the more established sections of Daybreak. The streets carry a mix of single-family homes and townhouses connected by paved trails, with direct access to the larger trail network that links residential streets to the central lake and commercial districts.

Where to Look for Homes

Focus your MLS search on Eastlake Village and the streets surrounding Overlook Park. You'll find mature landscaping here and immediate access to the community's eastern trailheads.

Housing density varies. Larger single-family homes tend to face the parks; townhomes sit closer to the main thoroughfares. Parking and lot sizes shift depending on which specific street you're targeting, so it's worth being precise when you're pulling comps.

Community Structure and HOA

Every property in Daybreak falls under a master homeowners association, which handles the parks, pools, community centers, trail maintenance, and landscaping around Overlook Park. The trails stay clear year-round - that's on the HOA.

What isn't automatic is the sub-association layer. Townhomes often carry additional fees for exterior maintenance on top of the master dues, and those assessments factor into your debt-to-income ratio during underwriting. Review the full fee schedule before you fall in love with a specific address.

Housing and Property Values in the Area

Property values in this section of South Jordan track the broader Salt Lake County market closely, though Daybreak's built-in amenities hold a steady floor. The overall South Jordan median sale price runs roughly $629,500, while the Daybreak-specific median hovered between $573,681 and $585,000 in mid-2026. That gap usually comes down to the high volume of townhomes and attached properties sold inside the master-planned community.

South Jordan currently has about 459 homes in active inventory - a 3.5-month supply. Homes are selling at roughly 98.9% of list price, which tells you sellers are pricing accurately and buyers are picking up minor concessions rather than significant discounts.

Available Property Types

The real estate mix near Eastlake Village and Overlook Park covers detached single-family homes, row-style townhomes, and some condominium units. Single-family homes here typically feature rear-loading garages and fenced side yards, which squeezes more usable square footage out of the smaller lots. Townhomes in this section of Daybreak usually run two or three bedrooms across multi-level floor plans, and they tend to move a bit faster - especially for buyers working closer to the $500,000 mark.

New Construction Versus Established Homes

The Eastlake area is one of the more established parts of Daybreak, so most of what you'll see in the MLS is resale rather than new builds. That's not a bad thing. Mature trees, finished basements, completed landscaping - you're not paying extra out of pocket to finish what the builder left undone.

If new construction is a priority, you'll need to look at the newer villages expanding on the west side of Daybreak. Buying near Overlook Park also means you're clear of active construction zones - no dust, no equipment noise on a Saturday morning.

Dining, Shopping, and Daily Commutes

Daybreak is designed so that everyday errands don't require leaving the community. Most of the commercial activity concentrates near Daybreak Parkway and District Drive. For residents near Overlook Park, groceries and a quick meal are a short drive or bike ride away.

Commuters typically use the Mountain View Corridor or Bangerter Highway to reach Salt Lake City, UT, or the Silicon Slopes tech hub in Lehi, UT. The TRAX light rail red line also provides a direct public transit option into downtown Salt Lake - which matters if you're trying to keep one car off the road.

Local Restaurants and Coffee Shops

Searching for restaurants in this part of Daybreak points you toward those main commercial hubs along Daybreak Parkway. Beans and Brews handles the morning coffee run. For sit-down meals, you've got Olive Garden and Village Inn. Quick meals come from Rumbi Island Grill, Cafe Trang Bistro, Jack's Donuts, McDonald's, Arby's, Chick-fil-A, and Firehouse Subs. The Break Pub/Cafe is the local go-to for evenings out without leaving the community.

Getting Around South Jordan

Inside Daybreak, the street grid is built around pedestrian and bicycle traffic - lower speed limits, roundabouts instead of signal intersections. Fern Ridge Drive and the other main connector streets link residential areas directly to the commercial centers.

Getting out is straightforward too. The layout drops you onto north-south commuter routes quickly, without grinding through miles of local traffic first.

Schools, Healthcare, and Local Climate

Daybreak is fully integrated into the regional utility and public service networks, so buyers aren't waiting on infrastructure to catch up with the development. That said, "integrated" means different things to different households - let me break down what's here.

The local climate follows standard Salt Lake Valley patterns: four distinct seasons, hot and dry summers, cold winters with real snowfall. The HOA manages snow removal on the primary trail systems, but your driveway and sidewalk are your responsibility.

Jordan School District Campuses

Every address in Daybreak falls under the Jordan School District. Public elementary options serving the area include Daybreak Elementary, Eastlake Elementary, Aspen Elementary, and Golden Fields Elementary. Middle school students typically attend Mountain Creek Middle or Copper Mountain Middle, and high schoolers feed into Herriman High or Mountain Ridge High.

Charter options are also available locally - Early Light Academy and American Academy of Innovation both serve the area.

Medical Facilities and Hospitals

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center sits right inside the community at 5126 W Daybreak Pkwy. It's not a satellite clinic - this facility includes a full-service 24-hour emergency room and AirMed flight transportation capabilities for critical cases that need transfer to the main university hospital.

For buyers with families or specific healthcare needs, having a major regional facility this close tends to move quickly from a nice-to-have to a genuine factor.

How to Start Your Home Search

With roughly 459 homes on the market across South Jordan, you have real options. Daybreak draws steady interest because of the community design and built-in amenities, so properties near Overlook Park don't sit forever. Knowing your budget and timeline before you start touring puts you in a much better position.

One data point worth keeping in mind: about 13.6% of South Jordan homes recently sold above list price. The market is balanced overall, but well-updated homes and properties with premium park views still pull competing offers.

Renting Versus Buying

Some buyers rent in the community first - it's a reasonable way to test the commute, live inside the HOA rules, and experience the amenities before committing your down payment. There's nothing wrong with that approach if the timing isn't right.

Buying locks in your housing costs and starts building equity in a stable South Jordan market. The honest move is to compare current mortgage interest rates against local rental prices and run the numbers for your specific timeline. One path isn't automatically better than the other.

Working With a Local Agent

An agent who knows the specific villages inside Daybreak will save you time immediately. The park and village names cause genuine confusion - buyers end up touring homes in the wrong section of the community more often than you'd think.

Beyond search accuracy, your agent will walk you through the HOA documents and sub-association fee schedules before you write an offer. You want to understand exactly what maintenance is covered and what your total monthly payment looks like before you're under contract.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Overlake Village in Daybreak known for?

While buyers often search for Overlake Village, the area is known as Eastlake Village, anchored by Overlook Park. This section of Daybreak features established single-family homes, townhouses, and clear mountain views from the park's eastern vantage point. It provides immediate access to the community's paved trail network.

What are homes like in Overlake Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

Homes near Overlook Park include a mix of detached single-family houses and multi-level townhomes. In mid-2026, the median sale price for the broader Daybreak community was between $573,681 and $585,000. Townhomes generally price slightly lower than the large, detached properties facing the parks.

How much are the HOA fees in Overlake Village, and what amenities do they cover?

HOA fees vary depending on the specific property type, with townhomes typically carrying additional sub-association dues for exterior maintenance. The master HOA covers the upkeep of Overlook Park, the community trail systems, and the shared recreational facilities. Buyers should review the specific fee schedule for any home they consider.

What restaurants and dining are in Overlake Village Daybreak?

Dining options are clustered along Daybreak Parkway and District Drive, just a short distance from the residential streets. Local choices include Beans and Brews, Olive Garden, Village Inn, Rumbi Island Grill, Cafe Trang Bistro, Jack's Donuts, McDonald's, Arby's, Chick-fil-A, Firehouse Subs, and The Break Pub/Cafe.

Which public schools are zoned for families living in Overlake Village Daybreak?

This area is served by the Jordan School District. Local public schools include Daybreak Elementary, Eastlake Elementary, Aspen Elementary, Golden Fields Elementary, Mountain Creek Middle, Copper Mountain Middle, Herriman High, and Mountain Ridge High.

What hospitals and medical care are near Overlake Village Daybreak?

The primary medical facility for the area is the University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center, located at 5126 W Daybreak Pkwy. This location includes a 24-hour emergency room and offers AirMed flight transportation for severe emergencies.

What is the weather like in Overlake Village Daybreak?

The area experiences the standard Salt Lake Valley climate, which includes four distinct seasons. Residents can expect hot, dry conditions during the summer months and cold temperatures with regular snowfall throughout the winter.
