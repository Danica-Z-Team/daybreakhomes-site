The Truth About Founders Village vs Founders Park Village in Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

If you've been searching for homes in Daybreak and noticed listings labeled "Founders Village" in one tab and "Founders Park Village" in another, here's the short answer: they're the same place. Both names point to the exact same original village built in 2004 on the south side of Oquirrh Lake. Not similar neighborhoods. Not adjacent phases. The same one. Keeping this straight makes it easier to compare the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT.

The median home price in South Jordan, UT sits at roughly $630,000, with properties spending an average of 50 days on the market. Knowing exactly what you're looking at - the housing stock, the HOA structure, the naming quirk - puts you in a better position before you start making calls.

Location and Community Layout in South Jordan

This is the original Daybreak neighborhood, and it sits on the south side of Oquirrh Lake. Official marketing materials for the master-planned community go back and forth between "Founders Village" and "Founders Park Village," and local sources pick up the same habit. Neither name draws a boundary line that the other doesn't cross. They describe the same fully built-out section of the development.

What that means practically: if you're comparing two listings and one says Founders Village while the other says Founders Park Village, you're not comparing two different neighborhoods. You're comparing two houses. The area itself has mature trees and established sidewalks that date back to the initial 2004 construction phase, and the street layout was designed from the start to push foot traffic toward nearby commercial and recreational areas rather than cars.

Parks and Local Amenities

There's real green space here. Founder's Park at 11428 S Kestrel Rise Road, Firmont Park at 4823 W Firmont Drive, and a string of pocket parks - Peek-a-Boo Park, Silent Rain Park, Sunflower Park - scattered through the residential blocks. Residents also have access to Callender Square, Hillside Garden, and Founder's Garden. The Daybreak Community Center at 4544 W Harvest Moon Drive handles indoor recreation and fitness.

Proximity to Oquirrh Lake and SoDa Row

The southern shores of Oquirrh Lake are walkable from homes in this section, and the lake connects to a paved trail network that runs through the various Daybreak villages. SoDa Row, the community's main commercial and dining district, is close by as well. There's also a local landmark residents refer to as the "glass house" - hard to miss once you've been out there, and a useful reference point when you're getting your bearings.

Home Styles and Current Market Prices

The range here is wider than most buyers expect. Attached units start in the low $300,000s; large custom estates push past $2 million. The average asking price for a home in this village runs around $646,000, with an average price per square foot of $214. South Jordan as a whole is sitting at about 3.5 months of housing supply right now, so there's a moderate amount of inventory to work through.

Because this was the first village completed in Daybreak, the landscaping is more established than what you'll see in the newer phases - that's not nothing when you're comparing properties. You'll find single-family homes, townhomes, and condominiums throughout the neighborhood, and homes here are selling at nearly 99% of list price on average. That number matters: it tells you competitive offers aren't optional.

Architectural Details and Property Types

The design draws from the classical architecture of the Avenues in Salt Lake City. Builders used traditional and historic-inspired exteriors with deep front porches defining the streetscape. Garages are accessed via rear alleyways, which keeps the street fronts open to sidewalks and landscaping. That approach holds across both the detached single-family homes and the attached townhome structures - the street presence feels consistent throughout.

Pricing Trends for Buyers

If you're working with a tighter budget, start with the condo and townhome inventory, which generally opens in the $300,000 range. You get the same exterior architectural standards as the larger homes without the larger price tag. Single-family homes make up most of the $600,000 to $800,000 band.

With an average of 50 days on market in South Jordan, you're not under the gun the way buyers were a few years ago. That said, don't sit on a property that checks your boxes. Fifty days is an average - the right house doesn't always wait for you.

HOA Fees and Daybreak Association Rules

The Daybreak Community Association master HOA base fee is $144.50 per month for 2026. Every homeowner in this village is part of that master association, which covers the lake, trails, and community centers. There's no separate fee schedule that splits Founders Village from Founders Park Village - again, same neighborhood, same fees.

What does vary is your total monthly obligation, which depends on property type and the specific construction phase. Pull the HOA disclosures during due diligence and verify the exact number for the address you're considering. For single-family homes, the typical all-in range across HOA layers runs $130 to $175 per month.

Phase 1 Exceptions and Base Fees

For most single-family detached homes in the village, the monthly HOA fee is roughly $121. Homes built during Phase 1 carry a slightly lower fee structure, and the reason is specific: high-speed internet is not bundled into the master HOA assessment for those earliest homes. If you're buying one of them, you'll set up and pay for your own internet service separately.

Townhome and Sub-Association Costs

Attached homes run higher - typically $200 to $375 per month depending on the specific building. That increase reflects a sub-association that handles exterior building maintenance and shared landscaping. When you're running your debt-to-income numbers, use the combined total of both the master association fee and the sub-association fee. Lenders require both amounts to approve the loan.

Researching Local Data for South Jordan Buyers

South Jordan maintains public portals for zoning codes and municipal statistics, and I'd encourage you to use them directly. Real estate agents can't make guarantees about future development or neighborhood crime rates - and you shouldn't rely on anyone who claims otherwise. Go to the official city databases, pull the data yourself, and you'll know exactly where it came from.

These resources are online and publicly accessible, so you can work through them at your own pace before submitting an offer.

Accessing Official Police Statistics

The South Jordan City Crime Reports and Statistics page is where you want to start for local safety data. The city publishes annual reports there for public review, along with direct links to the South Jordan Police Department's statewide crime statistics. That data runs through the Utah Bureau of Criminal Identification, which provides a standardized framework for comparing municipal reports.

Reviewing Zoning and Future Land Use

The South Jordan City Planning and Zoning Department hosts interactive GIS maps on its website. Use the South Jordan City Map Portal to look up the exact zoning designation for any specific address. The department also publishes Future Land Use Maps, which show what commercial or residential development is permitted on nearby vacant parcels in the coming years. If there's empty land near a home you're considering, that's the document to read.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between Founders Village and Founders Park Village in Daybreak?

There is no difference. They refer to the exact same original village built in 2004 on the south side of Oquirrh Lake. Official Daybreak marketing materials use both names interchangeably to describe this fully built-out neighborhood.

Which neighborhood has higher HOA fees: Founders Village or Founders Park Village?

They're the same neighborhood, so the HOA fees are identical. Your total monthly cost depends on property type - single-family homes typically run $130 to $175 per month, and townhomes range from $200 to $375.

What types of homes are available in Founders Village vs Founders Park Village?

The area has single-family homes, townhomes, and condominiums. All of them share a classical architectural style inspired by the Avenues neighborhood in Salt Lake City, with mature trees and large front porches throughout.

Do residents of Founders Village and Founders Park Village get the same access to Daybreak amenities like Oquirrh Lake?

Yes - same neighborhood, same access. The area is within walking distance of Oquirrh Lake, the Daybreak Community Center, and the shops at SoDa Row.

Are there different special assessment areas or property tax rates for homes in Founders Village compared to Founders Park Village?

No. Property tax rates and community assessments are the same because the names refer to the same geographic location. The only minor fee variation is in Phase 1 homes, which carry a lower HOA assessment because internet service isn't included.

Which of these two Daybreak neighborhoods has older homes, and what does that mean for buyer inspections?

Both names represent the original 2004 phase of the Daybreak development, making them the oldest homes in the master-planned community. Make sure your home inspector specifically evaluates the original 2004 construction elements during the due diligence period.
