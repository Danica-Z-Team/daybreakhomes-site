Buying a Home in Arrowhead Village, Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

Arrowhead Village is a distinct residential section inside the master-planned community of Daybreak in South Jordan, UT. It frequently ranks among the best neighborhoods in Daybreak. If you're searching the Salt Lake Valley, this corner of the market draws attention for its structured layout and the amenities that come baked into the Daybreak framework.

The broader South Jordan market is sitting at a median sale price around $630,000, with homes averaging roughly 50 days on the market. Knowing where Arrowhead Village fits within that picture is the first step to figuring out whether it's the right move for you.

Arrowhead Village's Place Within Daybreak

Daybreak is carved into multiple villages, each carrying its own architectural themes and streetscapes. Arrowhead Village is one piece of that larger South Jordan development - a sizable one, but still part of a community built around a deliberate master plan.

The design here leans hard into walkability and shared outdoor space. Trails and roads connect the villages to each other, so getting around doesn't always mean getting in the car.

Housing Setting and Layout

The streets in Arrowhead Village break from the uniform subdivision mold. Sidewalks and trail connections run between blocks, and rear-loading garages push the living spaces and front porches closer to the street - which changes the feel of the neighborhood compared to most of Utah's standard suburban layouts.

Shared alleyways handle the vehicle access in the back, which means the front of each home faces the street the way older neighborhoods used to. It's a small thing until you've lived in it, and then it's not small at all.

Connecting to the Wider Community

Buying here means you're joining the Daybreak Community Association. The HOA manages the shared parks, community centers, and lake access points that tie the whole development together.

Pull the association documents early - before you're emotionally attached to a specific house. The rules cover exterior modifications, landscaping standards, and how you can use the shared amenities. None of that is unusual for a master-planned community, but you want to read it before closing, not after.

Housing Styles and Property Costs

The wider South Jordan market is currently showing around 459 homes in active inventory. Arrowhead Village represents a slice of that - mostly attached townhomes and single-family detached houses. Recent figures put the South Jordan median sale price at roughly $630,000, and homes are closing at nearly 99% of list price, which tells you sellers aren't leaving much room to negotiate.

Properties in Arrowhead Village track with those broader city trends. Square footage and lot placement are the main variables that move individual prices up or down from the median.

Dominant Home Types

The housing stock here spans several phases of the Daybreak build-out, so you'll see a range of floor plans. Single-family homes tend to run modern farmhouse, craftsman, or contemporary on the outside.

Townhomes and condos are also in the mix. They carry a smaller footprint, and because the association handles most exterior landscaping and snow removal on attached units, the maintenance burden on the owner is lighter.

Market Pacing and Inventory

About 50 days on market gives you a reasonable window - not a frantic scramble, but not a leisurely browse either. Around 13% of South Jordan homes have recently sold above asking price, which means the well-priced ones are still drawing competition.

Get your financing pre-approval sorted before you start touring. The area is sitting at about 3.5 months of supply, which is balanced territory, but a good home at the right price will not wait around for you to pull your paperwork together.

Everyday Dining, Shopping, and Transit

Daybreak was designed so residents don't have to leave the community for routine errands or a meal. The commercial hubs are integrated into the master plan and connected directly to the residential blocks by the internal street network.

For commuting beyond South Jordan, the layout works in your favor too. The roads channel you out to the main thoroughfares without much fuss.

Local Restaurants and Retail

Daybreak's commercial centers hold local eateries, cafes, and service businesses - all built to be reachable on foot or with a short drive from the residential villages.

Larger grocery runs and big-box retail are out on the commercial corridors along the edges of South Jordan. The community's internal roads funnel you toward those corridors efficiently enough that it doesn't feel like a separate expedition.

Commuting and Transit Options

If you work outside South Jordan, highway access matters. Arrowhead Village residents connect to Bangerter Highway and the Mountain View Corridor through the local parkways.

Public transit is a real option here, not an afterthought. The TRAX Red Line serves the Daybreak area and runs a direct route into downtown Salt Lake City and the University of Utah.

Schools, Medical Care, and Local Weather

School zoning, healthcare access, and climate are the practical details that don't show up in listing photos but shape what daily life looks like. South Jordan has established public infrastructure for all three, and Arrowhead Village taps into that network as part of the broader Daybreak community.

Jordan School District Zoning

All of Daybreak - Arrowhead Village included - falls within the Jordan School District, one of the largest public school systems in Utah. Younger students are zoned for Daybreak Elementary and Eastlake Elementary. Middle schoolers feed into Mountain Creek Middle School, and high school is Herriman High School.

Nearby Hospitals and Emergency Care

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center sits at 5126 W. Daybreak Parkway and runs a 24-hour emergency department serving Daybreak and the surrounding South Jordan area. If something requires a full trauma facility, the University of Utah Hospital is the Level I Trauma Center referral for the region.

Climate and Seasonal Weather

Semi-arid, four distinct seasons. Summers are hot and dry; winters bring freezing temperatures and regular snowfall. That combination means annual property maintenance includes both winterization and snow removal.

The Daybreak Community Association handles clearing on communal paths. Your driveway and immediate walkways are your responsibility, so factor that into your seasonal routine.

How to Buy or Rent a Property Here

Arrowhead Village has both owner-occupied and rental properties, so buyers and tenants are working different angles of the same inventory. Either way, you need to track listings within the Daybreak community specifically - not just broad South Jordan searches - to get an accurate read on what's available.

An agent who knows the South Jordan market can help you calibrate pricing and walk you through the monthly HOA fees and property-specific rules before you commit to anything.

Touring Homes for Sale

Before you fall for a floor plan, review recent sales data so you understand where prices are landing. The $630,000 South Jordan median is a useful baseline, but where a specific home sits within Arrowhead Village matters too. Proximity to community parks, trailheads, or main roads shifts both the price and the day-to-day experience of living there.

Renting in the Community

Some investors buy in Daybreak to lease long-term, which means rental properties do turn up. Renting gives you a chance to know the neighborhood before deciding on a purchase.

One thing worth knowing: renters are held to the same association rules as owners. Leases typically require tenants to maintain the property exterior to the community's standards, so don't assume renting means fewer obligations on the upkeep front.

Frequently Asked Questions About Arrowhead Village

What is Arrowhead Village in Daybreak known for?

Arrowhead Village is a residential section within the Daybreak master-planned community. It's recognized for its mix of housing types, rear-loading garages, and direct access to the trails and shared parks that connect the broader South Jordan development.

What are homes like in Arrowhead Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

You'll find single-family detached houses, townhomes, and condos, with exteriors running modern farmhouse and craftsman styles among others. Pricing generally tracks the broader South Jordan market, where the median sale price is currently around $630,000.

What restaurants and dining are in Arrowhead Village Daybreak?

Daybreak's integrated commercial centers host local cafes and eateries within the community. A wider range of chain and independent restaurants is available along the main commercial corridors in South Jordan.

What is the weather like in Arrowhead Village Daybreak?

Semi-arid with four distinct seasons - hot, dry summers and cold winters with regular snowfall. Seasonal property maintenance is part of owning here.

What hospitals and medical care are near Arrowhead Village Daybreak?

The University of Utah Health South Jordan Health Center at 5126 W. Daybreak Parkway has a 24/7 emergency department. The University of Utah Hospital is the Level I Trauma Center referral facility for situations requiring a full trauma hospital.

Are short-term rentals or Airbnbs allowed for investment properties in Arrowhead Village Daybreak?

That depends on the Daybreak Community Association's specific regulations and the city of South Jordan's rules. Master-planned communities frequently restrict leases under 30 days, so review the HOA bylaws before purchasing - not after.

What schools are zoned for families living in Arrowhead Village Daybreak?

The neighborhood is served by the Jordan School District. Zoned public schools include Daybreak Elementary, Eastlake Elementary, Mountain Creek Middle School, and Herriman High School.
