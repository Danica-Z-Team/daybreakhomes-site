Zip Codes in Daybreak, UT: Boundaries, County, and Area Code Details for 2026

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

Daybreak is a large master-planned community located within South Jordan, UT. If you're buying a home in Daybreak, you'll want to get the postal details sorted early - utilities, school registration, and mail forwarding all depend on having the right zip code from day one.

The local market moves at a steady pace, with homes spending roughly 45 days on the market before going under contract. The median sale price across the South Jordan area sits at approximately $625,000, and buyers currently have about 447 active listings to choose from. Knowing the correct zip code also helps refine a property search and ensures accurate delivery to new construction sites.

The Primary Zip Code for Daybreak

All homes and businesses within the Daybreak community use the 84009 zip code - one code, full stop.

It covers the entire master-planned development. Whether a property sits near the northern edge or down by the newer southern expansions, 84009 is the designated mailing address. The United States Postal Service assigns this code specifically to the Daybreak portion of South Jordan, while neighboring areas outside the master-planned boundaries typically fall under different zip codes, such as 84095.

One dedicated zip code makes updating your address straightforward. It also means local delivery drivers immediately know a package is headed to Daybreak rather than the older sections of the city.

How Many Zip Codes Does Daybreak Have?

One. There are no secondary or overlapping codes for residential delivery within the official Daybreak boundaries.

That single-code system holds even as the development keeps growing. New phases and villages added to the master plan continue to adopt 84009 as they're built.

Areas Covered by the 84009 Zip Code

The 84009 zip code spans the entire footprint of the former Bingham Canyon (Kennecott) copper mine site where Daybreak was built. That's a lot of ground.

Major community hubs fall within this boundary - SoDa Row, the central shopping and dining district, and Downtown Daybreak, the newer sports and entertainment district that serves as home for the Salt Lake Bees. Transportation infrastructure is here too: the Daybreak Parkway and South Station TRAX light rail stations both operate inside 84009, giving residents direct transit access to the broader Salt Lake Valley.

Natural landmarks carry this address as well. Oquirrh Lake, the centerpiece of the community, and The Watercourse, a chain of waterways in the Upper Villages, both share the 84009 postal code.

Visualizing the Daybreak Boundaries

The western edge of the 84009 zip code generally follows the Mountain View Corridor.

On a map, the zip code takes a shape that closely mirrors the master-planned community's borders. The eastern boundary meets the rest of South Jordan and transitions to the 84095 zip code. Online mapping tools and real estate platforms use these boundaries to filter searches, so typing 84009 into a search bar isolates Daybreak from the surrounding South Jordan neighborhoods.

If you're looking at new construction, checking the zip code map confirms whether a subdivision is officially part of Daybreak - which determines access to community amenities like the Cove House and local info studios.

County and Metro Area Details

Daybreak sits entirely within Salt Lake County, UT.

That means the Salt Lake County government handles property taxes, deed recording, and certain municipal services for everything in the 84009 zip code. Property tax assessments run through the county assessor's office in Salt Lake City.

On a broader scale, Daybreak is part of the Salt Lake City-Murray, UT Metropolitan Statistical Area, which connects the local housing market to the economic drivers of the greater Salt Lake City area. It also falls within the larger Salt Lake City–Provo–Orem Combined Statistical Area - a regional classification that reflects the regular commuter traffic between Daybreak and major employment centers both north and south.

How Zip Codes Apply Across Daybreak Villages

Daybreak is organized into distinct villages, and every single one of them shares the 84009 zip code.

Established neighborhoods - Founders Village, Eastlake Village, and North Shore Village - use this postal code. So do Highland Park Village, Lake Village, and Garden Park Village. Springhouse Village, a 55+ community, along with transit-adjacent neighborhoods like South Station Village and Cascade Village, all share the same postal designation.

The newer west-side expansions follow the same rule. Creekside Village, Heights Park, and the Upper Villages and Watermark area near The Watercourse are fully integrated into the 84009 postal system.

Telephone Area Codes for the Community

Landlines and local businesses in Daybreak use the 801 area code. That's the original code for Salt Lake County and several surrounding counties along the Wasatch Front.

Population growth brought the 385 area code as an overlay for the exact same geographic region. If you're setting up a new landline or local cell phone number, you may end up with a 385 number rather than an 801. Both are equally valid here.

Both area codes require ten-digit dialing for local calls. If you have an 801 number, you'll dial all ten digits to reach a neighbor with a 385 number.

Finding Your Complete Nine-Digit Postal Code

The USPS assigns a ZIP+4 code to every specific street address in Daybreak. The base code - 84009 - directs mail to the general South Jordan post office, but those final four digits identify a specific delivery route, street segment, or even a single building. That extra precision speeds up automated mail sorting considerably.

You can find your exact nine-digit code by entering your street address into the USPS online lookup tool. That's especially useful if you're in a newer village where street names have only recently been added to the postal database. The full nine-digit code also reduces the chance of misdelivered mail and is often required when setting up accounts for utilities, insurance, or financial services.

You'll find a real mix of housing types in this zip code, from townhomes near the TRAX stations to larger single-family homes near Oquirrh Lake. About 15% of homes in the broader South Jordan area sell above the asking price. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official zip code for homes in Daybreak, UT?

The official zip code for all homes in the Daybreak master-planned community is 84009. This applies to every village, from Founders Village to the newer Upper Villages.

Do all Daybreak neighborhoods share the 84009 zip code, or do some use South Jordan's 84095?

All neighborhoods within the official Daybreak master plan use 84009. The 84095 zip code is used for areas of South Jordan that sit outside the Daybreak community boundaries.

Does buying a home in the Daybreak zip code guarantee my children will attend specific Jordan School District schools?

It depends on the exact address. While the 84009 zip code is entirely within the Jordan School District, specific school boundaries change based on which village you live in. You should check the district's boundary map for exact school assignments.

Are property taxes or HOA fees higher in the Daybreak 84009 zip code compared to the rest of South Jordan?

HOA fees in 84009 are specific to the Daybreak Community Association and vary by village and property type. Property tax rates are set by Salt Lake County and depend on the assessed value of the home, not just the zip code.

Will the newest Daybreak villages currently under construction keep the same zip code as the rest of the community?

Yes, new expansions like the Upper Villages and the Watermark area use the 84009 zip code. The USPS extends this postal code to cover the community as it grows.

How long does it take for a new construction address in Daybreak's zip code to register with USPS and delivery services?

It depends on the builder and the local post office. Once the street is paved and the home is near completion, the builder typically registers the address, but it can take a few weeks for the ZIP+4 to appear in the official USPS database.
