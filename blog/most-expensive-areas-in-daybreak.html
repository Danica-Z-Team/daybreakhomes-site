Exploring the Most Expensive Neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

The South Jordan, UT real estate market recently saw a median sale price of roughly $630,000, with homes spending about 50 days on the market. Within that broader area, Daybreak runs on its own pricing logic - community-wide median sale prices sit between $560,000 and $578,000, which already tells you something about the baseline before comparing the best neighborhoods in Daybreak.

But those numbers don't capture what's happening at the top. Waterfront estates and custom builds in the most exclusive sections routinely sell for well over $1 million, with private docks and lake views that buyers in the standard market never see.

A Look at Daybreak's Priciest Villages

The upper end of the Daybreak market concentrates around water. Townhomes in the community generally run from $470,000 to $600,000, and luxury single-family homes start in the $600s and push well past $1 million. The addresses that draw serious money aren't scattered across the development - they cluster in a handful of enclaves defined by limited lot counts and direct water access.

The Island at Oquirrh Lake

The Island at Oquirrh Lake is widely recognized as the wealthiest and most prestigious village in the community. It's a 160-lot island, entirely surrounded by water, marketed as the premier address in Daybreak - and honestly, the pricing backs that up.

Homes here consistently exceed the $1 million mark. Inventory is thin by design, and buyers often wait months for something to come available.

Lake Village and Eastlake Village

Beyond The Island, Lake Village and Water's Edge offer custom estates and luxury single-family homes, many with direct lake access and private docks. Eastlake Village has been an established section since 2006, and it shows - the homes here are among the largest in the community, with direct shoreline access. Median sale prices in Eastlake sit closer to $510,000, which, relative to The Island, reflects a different tier of waterfront living.

Income and Home Values in the Community

The median household income in the Daybreak neighborhood is reported at $120,520 - well above the national average of $79,597. Average household income runs even higher, at roughly $144,597. Those numbers track with what you see in the property values and the caliber of homes that sell here.

The broader South Jordan market currently holds about 3.5 months of housing supply, which gives buyers some room to negotiate on standard listings. Not a buyer's market, but not a frenzy either.

Defining the Luxury Price Tier

In Daybreak, luxury generally starts around the $750,000 mark. Daybreak Summit homes typically range from $750,000 to $1.2 million, while builder Rainey's Watermark Estate Collection runs from the $600s up to more than $1 million.

Currently listed luxury homes carry a median listing price near $575,000, but that figure gets pulled down by volume. Top-end listings push into the $1.5 million to $2.5 million range, and at that level you're looking at custom finishes and premium lot placement as the baseline expectation, not a selling point.

High-End Homes Currently on the Market

Active inventory in South Jordan recently sat at around 459 homes, with a portion of those in Daybreak's upscale villages. Homes here generally sell for about 99% of their list price - so if you're hoping to negotiate a significant discount on a well-priced listing, the data doesn't support that strategy.

The priciest properties aren't just larger versions of standard homes. They include features you don't find on typical luxury checklists.

Top-Tier Estates and New Builds

One of the most expensive homes listed in Daybreak sits at 10624 S Serendipity Way. It's a 7,293-square-foot estate priced at $2,559,000, with six bedrooms and six bathrooms.

The Serendipity Way property includes a private spa with a steam shower and sauna, a private home cinema, and an attached block of small retail shops. That last detail alone tells you how different the upper tier is from everything else in the community.

Matching the Right Area to Your Lifestyle

Daybreak is divided into distinct villages, each with its own mix of amenities and home styles. Condos start in the high $300s, townhomes cover the middle range, and large single-family estates occupy the top end - all within the same master-planned development, which is either a feature or a complexity depending on what you're looking for.

The variety is real. So is the importance of getting specific about which village fits your daily routine before you start making offers.

Access to Parks and Trails

The Lower Villages surround the 67-acre Oquirrh Lake, built in 2005, which anchors the community with miles of trails, parks, and a Beach Club. Buyers who want established outdoor recreation with some history to the neighborhood tend to gravitate here.

The Upper Villages offer something newer. The Watercourse - a mile-long network of channels and ponds - fully opened in 2024, giving residents in that section a different style of waterfront living. It's worth understanding which version appeals to you before narrowing your search.

Low-Maintenance Living Options

Not everyone buying at the higher end of Daybreak wants the upkeep that comes with a large custom estate. Luxury townhomes and condos throughout the community give you access to the same lakes and trails without that responsibility. Higher-priced condos still deliver premium finishes and proximity to community hubs - the trade-off is square footage, not quality. Review the specific amenities available in each village before you decide which format makes sense for your situation.

Crime Data and Cost of Living

Specific crime data broken down by individual Daybreak village isn't available, but the community-wide picture is clear. Daybreak carries a CAP Index Crime Score of 2 out of 10, compared to the national average of 4. A score of 2 means the area experiences lower crime rates than the average neighborhood in the United States.

Community Crime Scores

That score reflects what you'd expect from a structured planned suburban development. That said, always pull current local police reports for specific streets - aggregate scores don't tell you everything.

The broader cost of living here includes property taxes and community fees, and the median household income of $120,520 gives you a sense of the financial profile of people who live in this area.

Geographic Layout of Upscale Areas

Daybreak sits in South Jordan, UT, organized around its central water features and trail networks with straightforward access to the broader Salt Lake City metro area. The community divides roughly into Lower Villages and Upper Villages, and while both sectors carry a mix of housing types, the most expensive custom estates cluster near the water.

That geography matters more than it might seem when you're deciding where to focus your search.

Lower and Upper Villages

The Lower Villages include Oquirrh Lake, Eastlake Village, and The Island. The landscaping is mature, the Beach Club is right there, and the neighborhood character is established.

The Upper Villages are newer and anchored by The Watercourse. If you're drawn to new construction, this is where most of that activity is happening.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the richest part of Daybreak?

The Island at Oquirrh Lake is widely considered the wealthiest village in the community. It's a 160-lot island development completely surrounded by water, with some of the most expensive homes in the area.

Is Daybreak, UT a wealthy area?

The median household income is $120,520, which is well above the national average. The community also includes numerous luxury estates priced over $1 million.

What is the most expensive house in Daybreak, UT?

One of the most expensive listings is a 7,293-square-foot estate at 10624 S Serendipity Way, listed at $2,559,000. It includes a private home cinema, a spa, and attached retail shops.

What are the safest neighborhoods in Daybreak?

Is Daybreak a good place to live?

Depends on what you're looking for. Buyers who want extensive amenities, man-made lakes like Oquirrh Lake, and a structured master-planned environment often find it fits well. It's not for everyone, but it's well-suited to buyers who know what they want from a community.
