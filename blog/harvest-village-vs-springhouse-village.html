Harvest Village vs. Springhouse Village in Daybreak, UT: A Real Estate Comparison

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

The South Jordan, UT real estate market is sitting at a median sale price of roughly $630,000 right now, with homes averaging about 50 days on the market. For buyers focused on active adult or 55+ living, that search tends to narrow pretty quickly to the best neighborhoods in Daybreak, UT.

Here's the thing, though - when people ask me about Harvest Village versus Springhouse Village, I have to stop them before we get very far. There's a naming issue worth sorting out first. Springhouse Village is a real, dedicated 55+ neighborhood inside Daybreak's master-planned boundaries. "Harvest Village," as a Daybreak village, doesn't exist. Most buyers asking about it are thinking of Harvest Villas - a separate community that was built outside Daybreak entirely. Once you know that, the comparison looks a lot different.

Neighborhood Boundaries and Home Styles

Springhouse Village opened in 2018 as Daybreak's official 55+ community. It sits fully within the master-planned footprint, which means residents are part of the Daybreak Community Association, and all the homes here are built exclusively by OakwoodLife, a Berkshire Hathaway subsidiary.

Harvest Villas is a different story. Peterson Homes built it, and it sits outside Daybreak's official boundaries. That's not a minor detail - it means no Daybreak HOA dues, no Daybreak village zoning, and a fundamentally different relationship to the master plan. If being woven into that network matters to you, that distinction is worth thinking through before you start scheduling tours.

Homes in Springhouse Village

Springhouse Village homes generally list in the $400s to $600s, which puts the entry point below the broader South Jordan median. Oakwood Homes offers 11 floor plans here, and depending on timing, you'll find a mix of newer builds and recent resales on the market.

Homes in Harvest Villas

Harvest Villas runs on its own development timeline and pricing structure under Peterson Homes - it doesn't pull from Daybreak's data pool. The homes are designed for 55+ living, just without the Daybreak branding behind them. Have your agent pull recent Peterson Homes sales so you can compare square footage costs directly. That's the clearest apples-to-apples look you'll get.

HOA Fees and Community Amenities

The Daybreak Community Association is one of the more active HOAs in Utah - it manages a substantial network of parks, lakes, and fiber internet, and Springhouse Village residents are plugged into all of it. Specific dollar amounts for dues vary by property type and phase, so you'll want current figures from the association directly.

Harvest Villas residents are outside that system. No Daybreak assessments, but also none of the included maintenance or private amenity access that comes with those fees. Put the monthly numbers for both developments side by side before you decide what's worth what to you.

The Springhouse Village Clubhouse

Springhouse Village has its own private 10,000-square-foot facility called The Spring House. It includes a fitness center, pool, spa, pickleball courts, a game room, a golf simulator, and a demonstration kitchen used for community events. It's reserved for residents - not shared with the broader Daybreak population.

Broader Daybreak Access

On top of the private clubhouse, Springhouse residents can use Daybreak's trail network and Oquirrh Lake. Harvest Villas sits close enough to reach South Jordan's public commercial areas without trouble, but the private Daybreak amenities are restricted to HOA members. That line is firm.

Location, Commuting, and Accessibility

Daybreak is bordered by Bangerter Highway and the Mountain View Corridor, which makes getting around the Salt Lake Valley reasonably straightforward. Springhouse Village is on the west side of Bangerter Highway - directly adjacent to a major transit corridor for quick trips north or south.

Springhouse residents are also close to Daybreak's commercial anchors, including Downtown Daybreak and SoDa Row, with easy access to local shopping, dining, and the LiveDaybreak summer concert series. Harvest Villas is nearby, but reaching those central Daybreak hubs does require a slightly different drive. Not a dealbreaker for everyone, but worth knowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference in home prices between Harvest Village and Springhouse Village in Daybreak, UT?

Springhouse Village homes typically list in the $400s to $600s. Because Harvest Village (Harvest Villas) is not an official Daybreak neighborhood, it doesn't share Daybreak's pricing data, which averages around $525,000 overall. Check current Peterson Homes listings for a direct cost comparison.

How do the HOA fees and amenities compare in Harvest Village vs Springhouse Village?

Springhouse Village residents pay into the Daybreak Community Association and get access to a 10,000-square-foot clubhouse with a pool, pickleball courts, and a golf simulator. Harvest Villas is outside Daybreak, so it has its own separate HOA and doesn't include access to Daybreak's private amenities.

Is Springhouse Village strictly a 55+ active adult community, and how does that affect who can buy there compared to Harvest Village?

Yes - Springhouse Village is a dedicated 55+ community within Daybreak. Harvest Villas is also geared toward active adult living, but it operates independently of Daybreak's age-restricted zoning.

Can Harvest Village residents access the exclusive Springhouse Village clubhouse and amenities in Daybreak?

No. The Spring House clubhouse and Daybreak's private amenities are reserved for residents who pay into the Daybreak Community Association. Harvest Villas residents are outside those boundaries.

Which village offers better commuter access to the Mountain View Corridor and TRAX stations: Harvest or Springhouse?

Springhouse Village is located directly on the west side of Bangerter Highway, with immediate access to major commuting routes. Harvest Villas is nearby, but Springhouse is integrated directly into Daybreak's master-planned transit layout.

Are the available homes in Harvest Village and Springhouse Village mostly new construction or established resales?

Springhouse Village opened in 2018, so you'll find a mix of newer builds and recent resales across its 11 floor plans. Harvest Villas is also relatively recent, but inventory depends entirely on what phase Peterson Homes is in and how much turnover there's been.
