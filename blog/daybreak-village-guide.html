Daybreak Village Guide: Which Village Fits You

What are the villages in Daybreak, Utah? Daybreak, South Jordan is organized into several villages, including Garden Park, SoDa Row, Highland Park, Eastlake, Creekside, and Founders Park, each with a distinct feel and home mix.

People ask us the same question in different words: "Which part of Daybreak should I actually live in?" There's no single right answer, it depends on how close you want to be to the lake, how much you'll use the trail system, and whether you want to walk to dinner or drive five minutes for it. Here's how we'd describe each village to a friend.

Garden Park

Garden Park is one of Daybreak's more established villages, with a mix of single-family homes and a settled, front-porch feel. It's the kind of area where you recognize your neighbors' cars in the driveway.

SoDa Row

SoDa Row is Daybreak's gathering place: coffee shops, restaurants, and community events within a short walk of nearby homes. If you want groceries, dinner, and a coffee run all walkable, homes near SoDa Row are worth a look.

Highland Park

Highland Park offers a mix of home styles with easy access to Daybreak's trail network. It tends to suit buyers who want a quieter street but still want to be a short bike ride from the lake.

Eastlake

Eastlake sits closer to Oquirrh Lake itself, appealing to buyers who want water views or quick trail access as a daily habit rather than an occasional outing.

Creekside

Creekside leans toward townhomes and a lower-maintenance lifestyle, a fit for buyers who want the Daybreak lifestyle without a large yard to manage.

Founders Park

Founders Park rounds out the community with its own mix of product types and price points, often a good starting point for buyers still comparing villages.

How to Choose

Rather than picking a village from a map, we'd rather drive you through two or three that match your budget and lifestyle, and let you feel which one fits. Daybreak's compact enough that a single afternoon of touring usually settles the question.
