Daybreak, Utah Real Estate Market Update

What are homes selling for in Daybreak, Utah right now? Among the Daybreak buyers and sellers we work with, the typical price point sits around $575,000, though individual homes vary by village, size, and condition (South Jordan, 84009).

If you've been watching Daybreak from the sidelines, you've probably noticed the market doesn't move the same way it did a few years ago. Pace has slowed in some villages and stayed brisk in others. That's normal for a community this size, and it's exactly why a single "average" number only tells part of the story.

Here's how we'd break it down if you asked us over coffee at SoDa Row.

Pricing Across Daybreak's Villages

Daybreak isn't one price point, it's several. Townhomes near Eastlake and Creekside tend to sit at a different range than single-family homes in Garden Park or Highland Park. Founders Park brings its own mix of product types. If you're comparing your home (or a home you want to buy) against "the Daybreak market," make sure you're comparing it against the right village, not the whole community.

What Moves the Number

Lot size, distance to Oquirrh Lake and the trail system, upgrades, and how recently a home has been renovated all shift a property up or down from that $575,000 middle. Two homes on the same street can price a hundred thousand dollars apart once you factor those in.

Pace: How Fast Homes Are Moving

Days on market in Daybreak shifts with the season, with spring and early summer typically busier than the winter months. Rather than quote a single number that's out of date by the time you read this, we pull current days-on-market data for your specific village when you call us. It's a five-minute conversation that saves you from pricing (or offering) based on old information.

Inventory: What's Actually Available

Inventory in Daybreak tends to be tighter in the villages closest to the lake and SoDa Row, and a bit more open further out. If you're selling, that scarcity can work in your favor. If you're buying, it means being ready to move when the right home in the right village comes up.

What This Means If You're Selling

Pricing a Daybreak home well means starting with real comps from your village, not a citywide average. We build that comparison before we ever suggest a list price, so you're not guessing and you're not leaving money on the table.

What This Means If You're Buying

Getting a straight answer on what a Daybreak home is actually worth, before you write an offer, protects you either way. We'd rather tell you a home is priced high and why than let you find out after closing.

For broader context beyond Daybreak, sources like Redfin, Realtor.com, and UtahRealEstate.com track Salt Lake County trends more broadly. We use those alongside our own Daybreak-specific comps.
