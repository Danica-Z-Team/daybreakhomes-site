Real Estate and Daily Life in the Harvest Village Area of Daybreak, UT

By Tamara Zander, Real Estate Agent, Zander Real Estate Team  ·  Updated October 2026

Daybreak is a massive master-planned community in South Jordan, UT, and buyers searching for the best neighborhoods in Daybreak often look specifically for homes near Harvest Moon Drive or the 55-and-older Harvest Villas. "Harvest Village" isn't an official neighborhood name on the community map - the area around Founders Park and Garden Park is what people are referring to, and it functions as a central hub for that part of the community.

The broader South Jordan real estate market currently shows a median home price of roughly $630,000, with homes spending about 50 days on the market. In this specific section of Daybreak, you'll find a mix of single-family homes and townhomes built around community centers, local schools, and extensive trail networks.

What to Expect in the Harvest Area of Daybreak

The "Harvest" designation generally points buyers to two specific sections of the Daybreak master plan. The first is the corridor along Harvest Moon Drive within Founders Park Village, which anchors the northern end of the community. The second is Harvest Villas, a dedicated 55-and-older community located within Garden Park Village.

Both locations sit right on Daybreak's defining outdoor features. The entire community is organized around the 65-acre, man-made Oquirrh Lake, and residents have access to 22 miles of trails connecting residential streets to the water and surrounding parks.

For daily travel, Harvest Moon Drive, Lake Avenue, and South Jordan Parkway handle neighborhood traffic. Regional trips toward Salt Lake City go out via Bangerter Highway (UT-154) on the eastern edge or the Mountain View Corridor (SR-85) running nearby.

Real Estate and Housing Costs

The median sale price for South Jordan, UT as a whole is around $630,000. Zoom into the Daybreak area specifically and that number drops to roughly $574,000. Homes in South Jordan are currently spending about 50 days on the market before going under contract.

What you're buying depends on which part of the Harvest area you're targeting. Founders Park has established single-family homes and townhomes. Harvest Villas is built around single-level living for the 55-and-older market. Across the broader South Jordan market right now, there are about 459 active listings and 3.5 months of supply - enough inventory that you won't feel like you're making decisions under a gun.

Homeowner Association Fees and Rules

Daybreak runs on a layered HOA structure, not a single neighborhood association. Every owner pays into the master Daybreak Community Association, which runs $144.50 per month in 2026. That covers parks, trails, pools, the clubhouse, and access to Oquirrh Lake.

Most individual neighborhoods also carry sub-association fees on top of that. For single-family homes, total dues land roughly between $130 and $175 per month. Townhome owners typically pay between $200 and $375 per month. Two other items worth building into your budget: a $75 design review fee for exterior changes, and a rule requiring 12 months of owner-occupancy before you can rent the property to anyone.

Daily Life, Dining, and Local Amenities

The anchor for residents near Harvest Moon Drive is the Daybreak Community Center and Pool at 4544 W Harvest Moon Drive, right next to Founders Park. Most everyday shopping and dining happens a short drive or bike ride away at SoDa Row in Downtown Daybreak, which is where you'll find Tio's Mexican Restaurant, Porch, and The Break Sports Bar & Grill.

Restaurants and Coffee Shops

SoDa Row covers a solid range of casual options - Cupbop Korean BBQ, Maxwell's Pizza, and Sweet & Cool among them. For coffee, residents head to Ground To Earth in SoDa Row or make the trip to Biscotts Bakery & Café in Highland Park.

The nearby Trail Crossing Shopping Center handles the more routine stuff: Marco's Pizza, Arby's, and Beans & Brews for weeknight dinners and quick lunches.

Schools, Healthcare, and Weather

Northern Utah's climate is straightforward - hot, dry summers and cold winters with real snow. The trails and Oquirrh Lake see heavy use from spring through fall, and the associations handle regular snow management when the season turns.

For medical care, the University of Utah Health Center in Daybreak's South Station Village is the primary option. It's a 225,000-square-foot facility with a 24-hour emergency room and specialty clinics that include the Moran Eye Center and Huntsman Cancer services.

Jordan School District Boundaries

Public school students here fall under the Jordan School District. Daybreak Elementary - a K-6 school at 4544 Harvest Moon Drive - is the closest campus for many residents. Eastlake Elementary is another nearby option and runs a Mandarin immersion program.

Older students generally attend Elk Ridge Middle or South Jordan Middle, then feed into Bingham High or Herriman High. Attendance boundaries shift based on your specific street address, so check the official Jordan School District boundary map before you close - don't assume.

Finding a Home in the Harvest Area

Demand in this section of Daybreak holds steady. Founders Park properties attract one set of buyers; Harvest Villas draws another. The range is broad, and inventory moves.

The practical challenge is that "Harvest Village" doesn't exist as a search filter on most real estate platforms. You'll need to target specific streets or work with an agent who knows where the village boundaries fall.

Renting Versus Buying

Buying is the primary path into this area. Because of the Daybreak Community Association's 12-month owner-occupancy requirement, renting isn't an option immediately after purchase. If you're looking for an investment property to rent out from day one, you'll need to look outside Daybreak. If you're buying a primary residence, just make sure the layered association fees are built into your long-term budget from the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Harvest Village in Daybreak known for?

While not an official standalone village, the Harvest area is known for Harvest Moon Drive in Founders Park and the 55-and-older Harvest Villas in Garden Park. The area features immediate access to the Daybreak Community Center and connects to Oquirrh Lake via 22 miles of paved trails.

What are homes like in Harvest Village Daybreak, and what do they cost?

Homes include a mix of established single-family properties, townhomes, and 55-and-older single-level residences. The median sale price for the Daybreak ZIP code is roughly $574,000, while the broader South Jordan area currently sits closer to $630,000.

What restaurants and dining are in Harvest Village Daybreak?

The closest dining options are at SoDa Row, which features Tio's Mexican Restaurant, Porch, and The Break Sports Bar & Grill. The nearby Trail Crossing Shopping Center also has Marco's Pizza, Arby's, and Beans & Brews.

What is the weather like in Harvest Village Daybreak?

The area follows a standard Northern Utah climate - hot, dry summers and cold, snowy winters. Four distinct seasons dictate how much use Oquirrh Lake and the local outdoor community pools get.

What hospitals and medical care are near Harvest Village Daybreak?

The University of Utah Health Center is located nearby in South Station Village. This large clinic provides a 24-hour emergency room, Huntsman Cancer services, and the Moran Eye Center.

Are short-term rentals and Airbnbs allowed for investors in Harvest Village Daybreak?

No. The Daybreak Community Association requires owners to occupy the home for 12 months before renting it out to any tenants.

What public schools are zoned for families living in Harvest Village Daybreak?

The area is served by the Jordan School District. Local campuses include Daybreak Elementary, Eastlake Elementary, Elk Ridge Middle, and Bingham High, though exact assignments depend on the specific street address.
